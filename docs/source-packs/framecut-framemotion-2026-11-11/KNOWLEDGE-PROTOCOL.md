# From craft knowledge to an executable decision

Status: proposed knowledge maintenance protocol. This research edition contains source-supported guidance and calculated designs. It does not constitute a working standalone plugin or demonstrated native-editor expertise.

## A reusable unit

Use stable IDs such as `motion.transform.pivot` or `edit.trim.roll`; existing M/C/S labels are local research identifiers until deduplication. One canonical owner maintains the full unit. Another plugin can bundle a pinned, small original summary with an owner ID and digest; do not introduce a mandatory third plugin merely to read a concept.

Each unit must answer:

1. What does the viewer need to understand or feel, and in what context?
2. What evidence in the material supports the diagnosis?
3. What would an experienced operator inspect and change manually?
4. Which alternatives are viable, and what does each sacrifice?
5. What invariant must the agent preserve: meaning, coordinate basis, time, source identity, reading interval, color, or approval?
6. Which current engine can execute it, with which parameters and limitations? A proposed mapping is visibly different from existing support.
7. What counterexample defeats the rule or exposes a bad implementation?
8. What must be measured, watched or heard in the actual exported artifact before accepting it?

Keep source facts, attributed practitioner choices, imported user preferences and original synthesis separate. A practitioner interview supports that person's described practice; it does not prove that an unviewed scene has a particular rhythm. A vendor manual may contain contradictory wording. A source ledger must retain the relevant section/version/date and access depth, including excerpt-only and failed reads.

## Intake and correction

Imported material first receives an owner, provenance/rights disposition and review depth. Complete file inventory does not mean every sentence was verified. Retain private FCWM lessons, but convert useful general principles into original neutral guidance. Brand colors, fonts, transition preferences and accepted timing defaults remain named profiles. Do not redistribute manuals, source films or unlicensed examples.

Before adopting a claim, compare it with contrary evidence and one boundary case. When the old lesson conflicts with a later user decision, record both and the scope of the newer decision. A revision-bound one-shot waiver is different from removing self-review. A technical correction is different from changing the user's preferred aesthetic.

Use independent evidence axes: source read; calculation checked; engine exercised; native app exercised; perceptually reviewed; user accepted. They are not a single ladder or a percentage. Unheard sound remains unreviewed even if its duration and loudness pass. A mathematical fixture may validate a time transform without validating the resulting performance.

## Retrieval and applied evaluation

The future skill should route from the task's uncertainty to the smallest relevant packet. For a slipping reaction, load slip/roll/handles and sound synchronization; for a soft logo edge, load alpha representation/color boundaries. A recorded file read alone earns no craft credit. The proposed choice must cite the applicable condition and explain why a plausible alternative was rejected.

Before shipping a new unit, use an unseen brief not used to write its examples. Require a rationale, an editable proposal, a deliberately wrong comparison, and evidence from the encoded output. Separate technical reviewers from editorial/motion reviewers when feasible. Root or an author can verify arithmetic; neither can certify their own taste just by writing an approval field.

Essential negative scenarios include: a meaningful hesitation, a quiet title, an unreadable but correctly timed caption, a false implication created by juxtaposition, a wrong take with the same filename, a stale approved render, an alpha fringe, a twice-applied display transform, an accidental pause at a motion join, and a format change that preserves pixels but loses hierarchy.

## Stop conditions and limits

Stop research when the bounded question has a supported answer, a usable example and explicit uncertainty. Reopen it when a real exercise fails or the relevant engine/source changes. Do not grow bibliography counts as a proxy for expertise. The current edition deliberately leaves native AE/Resolve/Premiere/FCP/Avid operation, actual listening and cross-engine equivalence open; future validation requires real licensed media and observable results.

The product boundary remains the one in [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md). This protocol proposes how to preserve and use knowledge; it authorizes no repository creation, package migration, installation or release.
