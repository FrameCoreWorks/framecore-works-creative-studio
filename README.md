# FrameCore Works Creative Studio

[![FrameCore Works Creative Studio: Motion Graphics, FrameCut Editing, Storyboards, UGC Ads, Ad Graphics, Copywriting, Music & Sound, Brand Identity, Learning Mode, Open Assets](assets/banner/creative-studio-banner.gif)](assets/banner/creative-studio-banner.mp4)

Creative Studio is a plugin for ChatGPT Work, Codex and Claude that works with you on creative projects: ideas, ads, videos, motion graphics, graphics, writing, music and sound. You choose and direct; Studio plans the work with you, and where your assistant can run code it also makes the finished file.

## What Studio can do

- **Graphics and advertising:** posters, flyers, banners, social posts and campaign looks.
- **Video and motion graphics:** reels, ads, animated type, explainers, storyboards and shot plans, rendered as MP4 where code can run.
- **FrameCut:** [edit your own video](docs/framecut/README.md) with captions, animation and sound in one timeline.
- **Writing:** headlines, slogans, descriptions and dialogue in your audience's language.
- **Prompts and references:** instructions for image and video generators, with references that guide the project.
- **Music, voice and sound:** music ideas, lyrics, voice-over and sound design matched to the picture.
- **Learning mode:** a simple plan, short lessons, exercises and feedback on your own work.
- **Teacher Studio:** lesson scenarios, worksheets, quizzes and games for teachers.

Studio speaks your language and starts with a short menu: creative mode, learning mode, a skill finder, recommended repositories and FrameCut. A concrete request skips the menu. Generating images, video or sound uses the tools and accounts you already have; Studio bundles no paid service.

The [full feature list](docs/features.md) describes every part in detail.

## Install

**No terminal needed.** Paste this repository's link into ChatGPT Work, Codex or Claude Code (desktop, web, cloud or phone) and ask it to install Studio and explain each step; the [prompt at the top of each guide](CLAUDE_INSTALL.md#paste-this-prompt) says it in one sentence. The assistant installs Studio, asks whether to add FrameCore Open Assets, adds FrameCore Works Toolbox and shows a numbered list of recommended tools, split into those that work right away and those that need your computer.

## Companions

- [FrameCore Open Assets](https://github.com/FrameCoreWorks/framecore-open-assets): free, licence-checked photos, video, music and sound to use in your projects.
- [FrameCore Works Toolbox](https://github.com/FrameCoreWorks/framecore-works-toolbox): reviewed tools that extend Studio, with the built-in Earshot sound engine.
- Voice And Narration and Brand Kit: measured voice-overs, and a brand kit read from a website. They come from this repository's marketplace.
