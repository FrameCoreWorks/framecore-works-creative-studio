# Creative Studio 1.60.0

Vertical ads that are ready to brand and multiply.

- **Three ad templates.** An offer, a product and a customer recommendation, each 15 seconds for Stories and Reels, with feed versions in 4:5 and 1:1. Replace the placeholder text with yours and they render as they are.
- **Your brand and your languages.** Give Studio a client's brand kit and it applies the colours and the bundled font, warns about weak contrast, and turns one approved ad into versions in other languages and formats.
- **Captions that follow the voice.** A bold caption style shows whole words and lights up each word as it is spoken.
- **Sound that works on phones.** Mixes now keep presence on small speakers and report how much quieter they play on a phone.

The welcome, menus and all 37 skill IDs are unchanged. See [verification](VERIFICATION.md) and [release status](RELEASE_STATUS.md).
