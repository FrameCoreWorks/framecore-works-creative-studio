# Creative Studio 1.165.0

Before a reel or video, Studio now asks once how to go: control frames first (the default, also when the user does not answer) or the full render right away. For a video generator, full render gives the frame and video prompts together. In both modes Studio still checks its own frames (text fully visible, nothing covered or cut, safe areas, readable holds) before delivery, and paid or long generation is still asked first. The Toolbox companion pin moves to 1.6.1.
