# Creative Studio 1.124.4

Sound effects are now chosen by listening. After the owner listened to a test film and heard a metallic, rattling tail on a landing hit taken from a preset, Earshot Audio 1.0.4 and Studio pick every effect from a description of what happens on screen: they compare several candidates, reject those that sound like metal, rattle or glass or that a phone speaker would barely play, and report the readings and the rejected candidates. Your ears still decide taste.

Verification: source tests and Claude Code runs with Earshot's full installation before and after (case C17), recorded in verification/host-evals. ChatGPT Work and Codex remain NOT_RUN; the hosted plugins are updated separately.
