# Creative Studio 1.71.0

More motion graphics know-how, and lyric videos that follow the song.

- **A motion graphics compendium.** What works in Meta and short-form ads, which animation tools fit which job and what their licences allow, how to combine AI-generated images and clips with motion graphics, named techniques and transitions, and the fixes for the most common problems.
- **Lyrics timed to the song.** Give Studio a track and its lyrics: every word gets its sung time, each line gets a board cut that lands on the beat when it is close, and you get LRC and SRT files too. Speech recognition is installed on your computer when you need it.

The welcome, menus and all 37 skill IDs are unchanged. See [verification](VERIFICATION.md) and [release status](RELEASE_STATUS.md).
