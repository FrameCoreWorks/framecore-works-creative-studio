# Creative Studio 1.180.0

FrameMotion and FrameCut now come from their own marketplaces, so they are never installed twice, and installing Studio from the terminal brings the whole package: Studio, Toolbox, FrameMotion and FrameCut. In ChatGPT Work Studio is installed first and its onboarding asks about the sub-plugins.

- After an update Studio can clean up what an older version left in its folder: only files Studio itself shipped, only after you agree, with a list of what was removed.
- Reels follow one order: plan, contact sheet, a 540p preview, then the full render.
- Music for reels is made in code, because platforms mute or claim library music even with a free licence.
- Companions: FrameMotion 0.1.2, FrameCut 0.1.1, Toolbox 1.15.0, Open Assets 0.8.0.

Coming from 1.178.0 or 1.179.0 in Claude Code: add the `FrameCoreWorks/framemotion` and `FrameCoreWorks/framecut` marketplaces and replace the old copies as [CLAUDE_INSTALL.md](CLAUDE_INSTALL.md#3-take-inventory-before-installing) says.
