# Creative Studio 1.58.0

Motion graphics with real type.

- **What you approve is what you get.** Motion videos can use the bundled fonts, and the preview and the final MP4 now use the same font files, so a word that fits in the preview fits in the video.
- **Four new looks.** Sale poster for promotions, Editorial serif for food and craft, Product light for apps and SaaS, Bold grotesque for events and lifestyle brands.
- **Fuller vertical videos.** One setting enlarges a 9:16 layout so content fills the phone screen instead of floating in empty space.

The welcome, menus and all 37 skill IDs are unchanged. See [verification](VERIFICATION.md) and [release status](RELEASE_STATUS.md).
