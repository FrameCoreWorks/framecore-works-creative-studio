# Creative Studio 1.72.0

Steadier lyric timing, motion techniques by name, and print-ready PDFs.

- **Lyric timing fixes.** A song that starts singing at 0 s no longer gets a cut before the start, and words the recogniser missed between overlapping words no longer end before they begin; such lines are flagged for a listen. 29.97 fps projects (30000/1001) are supported.
- **Motion techniques by name.** When a brief says "morph match cut" or "light sweep", Studio looks it up in an atlas of 637 named techniques: what it does, when it fits, what pairs with it, and which HyperFrames block may already build it. The motion itself is still written for your brief.
- **Print-ready PDFs.** Studio can set trim and bleed boxes, embed your printer's colour profile and check a PDF before it goes to print: bleed, image resolution, colours and total ink, fonts, transparency, hairlines and attachments.
- **Script and campaign patterns.** One shared guide for scripts, short videos, campaigns, launches and sales conversations.

The welcome, menus and all 37 skill IDs are unchanged. See [verification](VERIFICATION.md) and [release status](RELEASE_STATUS.md).
