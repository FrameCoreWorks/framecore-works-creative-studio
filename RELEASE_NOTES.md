# Creative Studio 1.76.0

Fixes from an independent evaluation.

- **No invisible text.** A price or line placed outside the page now stops the export instead of silently disappearing; long fixed lines and highlighted caption words wider than the video are caught too.
- **Clearer offers.** Prices you enter with VAT are labelled as gross, and negative rates are refused.
- **New look, new approval.** Changing a client's colours or fonts on an approved motion piece asks for approval again.

The welcome, menus and all 37 skill IDs are unchanged. See [verification](VERIFICATION.md) and [release status](RELEASE_STATUS.md).
