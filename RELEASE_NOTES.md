# FrameCore Works Creative Studio 1.133.0

Choose how Studio starts. At first configuration Studio asks once: **guided** (the complete welcome and step-by-step menus, the default) or **workflow** (Studio joins your own workflow quietly; type "menu" for the full welcome). Switch any time with "guided style" or "workflow style". Concrete tasks skip the welcome in both styles. The choice is saved only with your consent where the host has real per-user memory; otherwise it lasts for the conversation and Studio gives you one line to paste into your settings.
