# Creative Studio 1.41.0

Studio finds the right skill more often and knows more where it was thin.

- **Better skill matching.** Every skill's description now starts with what it does and names its neighbour, and all of them fit the listing budget a host may apply.
- **Readable captions.** Caption plans start from dated line, duration and reading-speed values for subtitles and burned-in captions, with SRT and WebVTT rules.
- **Honest UGC.** Creator-style scripts choose who is speaking first, use proven formats and timings, and include disclosure lines for paid relationships and AI presenters, with dated rules for Poland, the EU and the US.
- **Short-form stories.** Eleven structures for reels and ads, a causality check and a beat budget.
- **Nothing left unread.** Method files that no skill used before are now part of the steps where they apply.
- **Learning and teaching.** Motion and sound lessons can compare two short renders where code runs; a finished quiz can become an interactive check.

Startup, the welcome, menus and all 37 skill IDs are unchanged. See [verification](VERIFICATION.md) and [release status](RELEASE_STATUS.md).
