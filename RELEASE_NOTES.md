# Creative Studio 1.59.0

Names and numbers in motion.

- **Lower thirds.** Introduce a speaker, a founder or a guest with their name and role at the bottom of the frame, animated in and kept clear of platform buttons.
- **Bar charts.** Show sales, results or survey answers as bars that grow while their values count up, in Polish or another number format.
- **Nothing cut off.** If a name or a value does not fit, Studio stops and says so instead of delivering a cropped video.

The welcome, menus and all 37 skill IDs are unchanged. See [verification](VERIFICATION.md) and [release status](RELEASE_STATUS.md).
