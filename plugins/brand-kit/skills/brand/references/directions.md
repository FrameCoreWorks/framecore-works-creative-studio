# Directions after the audit

Offer these as a numbered menu in the user's language, recommend one, and hand the choice to the owner named here
with the kit path, its revision and `brand-audit.md`. Owners are Studio skills (`framecore-work-creative-studio:`)
unless stated. A direction can combine several rows; run them one owner at a time.

| # | Direction | Studio owner | What the kit and audit give it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | New website ideas: structure, hero, sections, copy, visual refresh | `marketing` for positioning and page plan, `copy-voice` for page copy, `static-graphic-design-creator` for hero and section visuals | Voice profile, current CTAs and promises, palette and fonts as the starting point or as what to change |
| 2 | Ads and Meta Ads: angles, audiences, creatives, test plan | `ecommerce-campaign-strategy-director` (its website-to-campaign route) for a store, product or offer; `marketing` otherwise; then `commercial-visual-campaign-director` and `static-graphic-design-creator` for the creatives, `copy-voice` for primary text and headlines | Offer and promises as observed wording to verify, the kit's colours, fonts and logo as locks |
| 3 | Video ads and reels | `commercial-video-campaign-director` for the idea, `creative-video-producer` to run the project, `hyperframes-workflow` for motion graphics built from code, `ugc` for creator-style ads | Kit colours and fonts for motion, voice for supers and voice-over |
| 4 | Brand development: positioning, identity refresh, logo system, brand guide | `marketing` with its brand identity workflow (`workflow-orchestrator/references/brand-identity-workflow.md`) | The audit's gaps and contradictions as the brief; the current kit as the baseline revision |
| 5 | Content and social: posts, carousels, a content plan | `marketing` for the plan, `copy-voice` and `static-graphic-design-creator` for posts, `caption-studio` for subtitles | Voice profile and kit locks |
| 6 | Spoken brand voice: voice-over direction and reads | `copy-voice` for the script, `audio-production-director` for voice direction, then the `voice-narration:narrate` plugin to generate and measure | Tone and address from the voice profile |

For a portable design specification for other design tools, Studio's DESIGN.md export
(`pipeline-core/assets/design-md/`) takes the saved kit.

Competitor comparison is not part of this plugin: Studio's `marketing` and `research-evidence` handle it when the
user asks.
