## Evidence levels

Structural validation checks files, manifests, links, routes, provenance hashes and critical instruction contracts. Mutation tests demonstrate that specific omissions or contradictions are detected. Planned behavior fixtures specify tasks and criteria; they do not execute tools or establish quality. Fresh-context textual runs can assess instruction-following on supplied scenarios but do not establish target-host retrieval or actual media quality. Real media review must retain accessible input/output, method, inspected range, tool result and applicable acceptance criteria.

The legacy oversized validator, test and fixture files were preserved unchanged because backend readback/archive did not provide their complete current contents. New canonical validation and explicit effective-eval overrides avoid overwriting those historical sources. No prior result is relabelled as a result of dev.29.
