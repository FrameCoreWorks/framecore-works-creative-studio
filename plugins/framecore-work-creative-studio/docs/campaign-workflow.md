# Website-led marketing and sales campaigns

Creative Studio can connect an accessible company/store website to a brand and offer diagnosis, incremental business questions, campaign strategy, asset briefs, requested production and a measurement plan. The existing Ecommerce Campaign Strategy Director owns the commerce plan; Marketing and the existing graphic, UGC and video owners supply their specialist work. No new role or provider is installed.

## Route

1. Read the supplied public URL and relevant sampled pages, or disclose an access limitation. Track observations, merchant claims, hypotheses and unknowns separately.
2. Ask one material missing question at a time about the business decision. Reuse supplied information; complete briefs go straight to the requested stage.
3. Establish the priority offer, buying situation, positioning, proof and claim boundaries. Prioritize opportunities against real business resources.
4. Propose a small campaign with distinct angles, channels, destinations, asset roles and staged rollout. Keep a single selected direction when already supplied.
5. Prepare asset cards for requested website banners, product/lifestyle images, social posts, carousels, Meta Ads and UGC/video. Preserve exact product and human-reference properties, copy and source revisions.
6. Produce only explicitly requested feasible assets through actually available authorized tools, inspect the outputs and deliver their real file/status evidence.
7. Define or interpret campaign metrics using actual comparable data. Keep inconclusive results and alternative causes visible; changes of spend or publication need their own authorization.

## Example requests

The following are synthetic task examples, not inspected businesses or executed campaigns.

- “Here is my store URL. Inspect its offer and ask me the next question needed to plan a social sales campaign.”
- “Use this supplied product information without browsing. Plan a small fragrance launch with website banners, static ads and a fictional presenter. Mark unsupported claims.”
- “The campaign direction and copy are accepted. Prepare only the next product-banner prompt from these reference images.”
- “Review these campaign exports, explain what their attribution differences prevent us from concluding, and propose the next bounded test.”

## Practical limits

A public page cannot reveal internal sales, margins, audience demographics or verified tracking. A merchant's claim is not independent substantiation. Synthetic presenters are not real customers. A production-ready prompt is not an actual asset; an inspected graphic is not evidence of conversion lift. Native or external execution depends on the current host, user authorization and actual source availability.

Current ad placement limits, account controls and disclosure rules need verification for the actual surface and market. The dated [source cards](../skills/ecommerce-campaign-strategy-director/references/campaign-evidence-sources.md) record what was and was not accessible. This update adds instructions and handoff methods, not a site crawler, analytics installation, Meta Ads connector or automated campaign manager.
