## Reusable materials

Each chapter links its own complete examples or templates. Image work includes reference-role and still-shot cards; design has exact-copy/format cards; story, voice, campaign, sequence and boards share the original fictional Ślad project; AV workbooks include complete prompts, performance briefs, cue/evidence and continuity templates. Delivery provides two manifest JSON assets and the optional Python helper. Critic and Research provide concise working records.

## Evidence and maintenance

[Research ledger](research-ledger.json) lists 27 primary works plus current provider/rights documentation and their scope. The relevant chapter gives the source-to-rule-to-example mapping and available publication/version dates. Additional leads and failed retrievals are excluded from evidence. No third-party media or long source excerpts are bundled. Current provider operations still require fresh operation-specific verification.

The deterministic helper checks declarations; fresh textual use checks instruction behavior. Neither demonstrates actual media quality, automatic skill retrieval in a new host conversation, licensing clearance or complete migration of earlier custom GPT sources.
