# Motion sound sources

The [motion sound library](../../skills/hyperframes-workflow/assets/motion-sound/README.md) bundles 79 recordings, unchanged, from four packs released under CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication). [source-manifest.json](source-manifest.json) records each pack's page, download URL, archive SHA-256 and the SHA-256 of every bundled file.

| Pack | Author | Files used |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Impact Sounds 1.0](https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds) | Kenney | soft, wood, glass, metal and plate impacts |
| [Interface Sounds 1.0](https://kenney.nl/assets/interface-sounds) | Kenney | clicks, ticks, switches, toggles, scroll |
| [UI Audio](https://kenney.nl/assets/ui-audio) | Kenney | clicks, mouse click and release |
| [Swishes Sound Pack](https://opengameart.org/content/swishes-sound-pack) | artisticdude | 13 swishes recorded from swung wood and metal objects |

The Kenney `License.txt` files are kept here unchanged. Credit is not required by CC0; crediting Kenney and artisticdude is still appreciated.

The sound-design method also draws on [video-shotcraft](https://github.com/Vincentwei1021/video-shotcraft) (Apache-2.0; methods only, none of its Mixkit-licensed audio) and [kaventro/motion-designer](../kaventro-motion-designer/README.md) (MIT). Rejected sources and the reasons are listed in the manifest.
