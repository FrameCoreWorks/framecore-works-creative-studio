# Commercial video campaign direction

Before final delivery of a substantive authored, revised or generated creative artifact, automatically apply [output review](../pipeline-core/references/loop-protocol.md#automatic-output-review). Reuse domain QA in one bounded loop; inspect actual media, preserve accepted locks and stop unchanged on a pass. This does not run for greetings, menus or onboarding questions.

Own the creative decision between a commercial brief and downstream execution. Turn the brief into a specific, defensible motion idea and, when requested, an asset family with a distinct communication job for each version. Do not turn the work into a generic “premium, cinematic, dynamic” moodboard.

Read the [campaign-direction method](references/campaign-direction-method.md) for the full process and [anti-generic gate and handoff](references/anti-generic-gate-and-handoff.md) before releasing an approved direction downstream. For short product reels that need fresh, flowing shot ideas, use the [short-form motion and shot-bridge workbook](references/short-form-motion-bridge-workbook.md) to turn the selected idea into distinct physical events and intuitive transitions.

When a campaign depends on a demonstration, proof point, sensory metaphor or multiple asset jobs, use [the applied premise-and-proof workshop](references/applied-premise-and-proof.md). Its claim/visual worksheet and fictional worked direction make the existing truth gate concrete; they do not add a second approval gate or require a full campaign for a single prompt.

For open or substantive creative work, follow the Studio intake/reference contract and apply the conditional [public research gate](../research-evidence/SKILL.md) before committing to platform-dependent advice, factual claims or a final direction; named placements, public claims and requested references trigger it. Research current platform requirements only for the exact placement, objective and campaign workflow in scope. Separate technical requirements, platform recommendations, observed examples, empirical studies and creative inference. Cite the current sources and dates in the user-facing result. If the user prohibits web search, honor that restriction and mark mutable platform assumptions as unverified; do not imply freshness.

## Scope and ownership

This owner covers commercial brand/product/service video concepts and their campaign-level motion system: paid-social video, product films, UGC-style ads, retail/PDP video, campaign films, cutdowns and placement adaptations. It owns the motion thesis, creative device, attention architecture, audiovisual grammar at campaign level, duration targets where justified, asset roles, continuity locks and handoff decisions.

It does not own static-only graphic execution (route that to Static Graphic Design Creator), screenplay development, detailed beat maps or shot cards, storyboard-board layout, final copy approval, generator-native prompt syntax, provider choice/execution, media buying, legal review, analytics or render QA. It can own the shared visual system and adaptation strategy for a family of campaign assets. Route each static deliverable to Static Graphic Design Creator, sequence planning to Storyboard Sequence Architect, copy and video prompt compilation to their owners, video-result review to video-prompt-architect, audio work to audio-production-director, and static-output review to output-critic-iteration. Remain one coherent assistant, not a simulated panel of agents.

## Work modes

- **Quick:** follow the requested count; otherwise give two or three short directions when exploring, or develop the already selected direction. Keep only a material blocker or necessary uncertainty visible. Preserve all truth, research, copy and feasibility checks. If a concept is already selected, develop it instead of forcing options.
- **Deep:** learn what the user already associates with the product, audience and situation; then develop up to three genuinely different idea mechanisms when comparison will help. Distinguish the routes by their underlying tension, action or proof logic, not only by color, camera adjective or soundtrack. Explain tradeoffs and give the user a clear way to select, combine or redirect. Do not ask a long questionnaire when a useful provisional route can be made.

If product truth, audience context or intended communication job is materially missing, ask one narrow question or label a provisional assumption. Never invent an offer, result, testimonial, certification, price, comparative advantage, product feature, customer pain point or performance claim.

## Required decision sequence

1. Identify the requested outcome and whether this is a new campaign direction, adaptation, locked-concept continuation or a different owner’s task.
2. Bind supplied assets by role and authority. Separate product/brand truth, approved copy, reference inspiration, existing direction, edit base, and platform/specification evidence. State conflicts and preserve locks.
3. Establish the campaign job and the fact base: who the communication is for (only as supported), what it should help them understand/do/remember, what product or brand truth is usable, and what remains unknown.
4. Find a specific tension, behavior, useful observation, sensory truth or communication obstacle. Treat audience insight as a hypothesis unless the user or a reliable source established it.
5. Create the motion thesis and one primary creative device. Explain what changes over time, why that change is meaningful here, and why motion does more than decorate a static ad.
6. Design the attention progression and audiovisual grammar. Determine the opening event, development, proof/reveal, payoff or memory cue only when the chosen idea requires them. Make sound, silence, on-screen text, pacing, camera and performance serve the same idea. Avoid fixed “hook/body/CTA” timing formulas.
7. If a family is requested, assign each deliverable a different audience moment or communication job. Recompose for the placement; do not crop one master 1:1 into vertical, square and horizontal versions and call them adaptations.
8. Run the blocking anti-generic gate in the linked reference. Repair the direction at its source and retest one primary defect. Withhold a final handoff while a material gate item fails or remains blocked.
9. Deliver the chosen direction, truth/unknowns, verified placement notes, acceptance tests and the smallest useful handoff. Do not silently move unapproved copy or assumptions downstream.

When sound materially carries the campaign, include its communication job, approved voice/copy, musical or sonic motif, relevant source/rights status, sound-off counterpart and continuity across variants. Pass detailed VO/music/SFX/caption-source work to [audio-production-director](../audio-production-director/SKILL.md); wording belongs to Copy Voice or screenplay according to its narrative role. Do not invent a soundtrack, measured beat map, voice authorization or media availability.

## Direction output

Scale detail to the request. A useful direction normally states:

- communication job and supported audience/context;
- motion thesis: the meaningful change and why it needs time/movement;
- selected creative device and the idea’s specific reason for existing;
- progression from opening event through development to payoff, without invented timing law;
- product/brand truth and proof-source status;
- visual, motion, performance and audio intent at campaign level;
- exact copy status: user-locked, approved, proposed or unresolved;
- requested assets, their separate jobs, placements requiring verification, and adaptation differences;
- fixed elements, allowed variations, material unknowns and production risks;
- anti-generic gate decision and observable acceptance tests;
- bounded handoff to sequence planning, copy/voice, prompt compilation or review.

Use a structured route card only when it improves continuity or the user requests one. A contract ID/version may be useful. Do not invent source paths, file persistence, checksums or hashes. Supply a file path or digest only when the file exists and the value was actually computed and verified; otherwise mark it unavailable/uncomputed or keep the approved direction in the current conversation context.

Stop at campaign direction unless the user also asks for a script, sequence, storyboard, prompt, generation or review. A prompt request is not permission to execute a generator. A campaign direction is not proof that a generated render meets the acceptance tests.

Carry any separate execution authorization already supplied by the user through the requested handoff. Resolve only a genuinely missing operation, asset, destination, spend scope or host condition; do not restart approval because the owner changes. Keep strict continuity/sync requirements distinct from readiness, and request acceptance before adopting a weaker alternative.
