# Applied campaign premise and proof

Use when a beautiful motion idea might imply a benefit the brief cannot support, or when several deliverables merely repeat the same ad. This original exercise operationalizes the existing direction gate. It supplies neither a performance forecast nor legal clearance.

## Keep four things separate

| Layer | Example question | Required treatment |
|---|---|---|
| Supplied truth | What does the client actually establish? | Carry source and limits; wording approval alone is not verification |
| Authored premise | What original situation or visual device could express the idea? | Label invention; do not present a fictional scene as a customer account |
| Planned demonstration | What would the film need to show? | State the observable action and source evidence needed |
| Demonstrated result | What does the actual inspected footage establish? | Record real scope; a storyboard or generated scene is not product-test evidence |

A metaphor is usable when its reading stays within the supported proposition. Test the combined image, sound and copy: does a glowing result, stopwatch, before/after or delighted testimonial imply a claim that the words avoid? Do not fix an overbroad main impression merely by shrinking a qualifier.

## Find the smallest meaningful change

Describe the first state, the action that changes it, and the viewer's resulting understanding. The change may be practical, relational or perceptual. It need not exaggerate product performance. If all that changes is a camera orbit around an unrelated object, revisit why this campaign needs movement. Preserve an intentional sustained image when attention or sound evolves meaningfully.

Use the [premise/proof worksheet](../assets/premise-proof-worksheet.md) only when the ambiguity is material. A missing exclusive selling point does not prevent an authored composition. A missing claimed capability does prevent treating that capability as fact.

## Give each requested asset a different job

Retain the campaign's memory cue, truth limits and exact selected copy. Then decide what each actual requested asset lets its viewer notice or do. A close-detail version can establish an observable mechanism; a contextual version can establish use; a static key visual can hold the memorable relation. Do not invent a funnel, placement, duration or extra deliverable.

Pass the selected thesis to static direction; pass sonic function and approved language to Audio Production Director; pass story to screenplay and timing/coverage to sequence. Video prompt compilation maps the execution surface. ## Apply platform advice within its scope

Google's [ABCD guidance for YouTube video ads](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/14783551?hl=en), accessed 2026-09-28, separates attention, branding, connection and direction, with advice varying by marketing objective. It is platform guidance, not a compulsory four-beat script. Use it to interrogate a requested YouTube ad's job and brand presence; do not import its reported performance lifts into a project forecast, turn branding advice into a universal timestamp, or impose it on an artist-led film.

The worksheet and proof tests above are original Studio synthesis, not research demonstrating effectiveness. A direction passes when its authored mechanism is specific, the implied claims remain supported, and its requested handoffs are usable. Render QA and outcome measurement remain separate.
