# Short-form motion and shot-bridge workbook

Use for a social reel, product film or short advertisement when the user needs a fresh sequence of short, product-specific visual events. This extends campaign direction into usable shot logic; it is not a formula for every platform or a guarantee of performance.

## Research for mechanisms, not imitation

For a substantive concept, use the shared Research Evidence gate before choosing a direction. Search the exact placement and category, then look beyond it for physical, visual or editorial mechanisms that can transfer without copying a source. When available, inspect complete videos or primary case studies rather than image thumbnails or short snippets. Record title/link/date, what is directly observed, what is your inference, and which choice the evidence changes.

TikTok for Business and Meta publish platform-specific creative guidance, case studies and libraries. Their recommendations and performance claims describe their own context and should be treated as vendor guidance, not universal creative law. For instance, TikTok recommends clear openings, sound and native feeling; Meta reports Reels-ad results under specified test conditions. Neither warrants forcing a fixed hook/body/CTA pattern or copying an ad. Follow the brief and use platform conventions only when they help the actual audience and placement.

## Quick pitch first

In Quick mode, give a few short directions only. Each should contain:
- a concrete visual event, not only a mood or list of objects;
- why that event belongs to this product/brief;
- one intuitive relation to the next shot or ending.

Do not produce a long beat sheet, full prompts, multiple reference boards or generation-ready asset package before a direction is selected unless the user already asked for the complete work. A pitch may state that it is exploratory. After an unexplained rejection, ask one targeted question about the missing quality before another ideation/search pass. If the user already states the correction, apply it directly. Do not guess repeatedly.

Deep mode develops the selected route in stages. Keep research, choice, sequence, reference design, model prompts and execution as separate decisions. Do not turn platform advice into a research essay unless requested.

## Build an imageable event and a flowing chain

Start from the supplied source image and verified product attributes. Identify which visible anchors must survive: silhouette, label, package proportions, surface, color, cap, contents, branding and use constraints. Distinguish real product claims from visual metaphors.

For every proposed shot, define:

1. **Starting state:** where product/subject is and what the frame shows.
2. **One primary action or perceptual change:** a pour, contact, reveal, reaction, transformation, camera move, shadow shift or other readable event.
3. **Ending state/payoff:** what has changed and what the viewer can now notice.
4. **Product reason:** how the event expresses a supplied product truth, tactile property or concept. Avoid a decorative event unrelated to the product.
5. **Shot construction:** scale, angle, lens feel, camera/subject movement, light, material response, setting and likely continuity risk.
6. **Bridge:** outgoing and incoming motion/shape/color/texture/axis/eyeline, action consequence, sound lead/tail or an intentionally motivated break.
7. **Generation evidence:** required reference and identity carriers, target model/surface research, and an observable QA test.

An attractive image is not automatically a useful shot. Reject or repair a shot when its action is unreadable, product truth is distorted, adjacent shots have no meaningful relationship, or the concept only works as a chain of unrelated AI spectacle.

## Score the sequence before compiling prompts

Use this review only to find the weakest link; it is not a rigid numeric creativity formula.

| Check | Pass question |
|---|---|
| Product fit | Would this action or material choice still make sense if the product were replaced? If yes, make it more specific. |
| Readability | Can a viewer understand the event without the explanation? |
| Flow | Does each cut connect, cause, echo, contrast or intentionally surprise with a useful reset? |
| Rhythm | Do shot lengths and accents serve the action/track, with room for a held image when it earns its place? |
| Originality | Are the ideas distinct from routine category images and from the exact references found? |
| Feasibility | Can the selected model, references, art direction and allowed tools plausibly realize the shot? |
| Continuity | Are package, material, person, props, light, axis and color changes intentional? |
| Payoff | Does the ending resolve, echo or transform the opening without inventing a claim? |

A low score identifies a repair question, not permission to polish every row. Select one primary repair and retest the affected bridge.

## Build the selected timing plan

For a target around 25 seconds with shots near 4–5 seconds, allow enough visual events to carry the runtime, but let the chosen action and audio define exact duration. Use measured frames/timecode when available; otherwise label all durations as targets or estimates. Sum the intended picture duration to the requested runtime and account for transitions/handles separately.

The selected shot cards can include:
- shot ID and in/out with timing evidence;
- purpose and visible starting/action/ending state;
- product/identity carrier and source references;
- composition, movement and light/color/material;
- precise continuity and transition relation;
- dialogue/copy/VO and music/SFX intention;
- intended model operation, current source date and known limitation;
- one acceptance test and one likely failure to check.

Ask the storyboard owners to create the actual ordered sequence and/or board when requested. A static reference sheet has different responsibilities from a time-based beat/shot map. Keep panels large and labels readable; maintain exact order, timing, action state, color/light, clothing, camera and reference roles. A contact sheet is not automatically a continuity proof.

## Prompt and sound handoffs

Only after the direction and shot functions are selected, pass the exact cards and reference roles to image/video prompt compilation. Search current official model docs and first-hand experiments for the exact version/surface. Do not attribute unverified prompt syntax to the model. A still board, reference sheet, prompt, render and inspected final shot are distinct states.

Pass sound to Audio Production Director in either direction: map an existing video to sound, or choose a musical pulse/section and design visuals around it. Keep audio and image clocks aligned, distinguish estimated BPM from analysis, and use sound bridges/accent moments intentionally rather than cutting on every beat.
