# Creative decision library

Use this chapter when the brief is clear but the proposed images feel interchangeable, ornamental or causally weak. It supports the existing campaign owner; it does not create another intake or force a full campaign. For a static-only deliverable, Static Graphic Design Creator remains the author and may borrow a relevant decision exercise. Narrative scenes stay with Screenplay; sound stays with Audio Production Director.

## Evidence and transfer

Read the [source cards](../../research-evidence/references/creative-upgrade-sources.md) for scope and dates. The four case notes below come from the makers' written project pages. Their films were not played or measured during this package update. Shot order, camera/lens choices, exact timing, performance and conversion results are Unknown unless separately inspected. Do not present a written case description as a shot-by-shot visual analysis.

Extract a decision principle, then design a different situation, action, setting and visual solution for the actual brief. A publicly accessible reference is not a licence to reuse its frames, music, logo or choreography. Prefer a relevant primary project page or original technical explanation; a generic social post, repost or search snippet is not evidence. Follow a user's source exclusions.

## Four documented production approaches

| Primary project | What its maker actually describes | Studio transfer, an inference | Boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| ManvsMachine, Nike Air Max Day '16 | Designer perspectives represented through kinetic sculptures. | Let a specific idea determine how an object moves; compare mechanisms before choosing surface styling. | Do not claim those sculptures physically demonstrate product performance. |
| BUCK, A Tiffany Holiday | One hero film and short vignettes centred on the Bird on the Rock brooch. | Preserve a distinctive asset while changing each short piece's event or payoff. | The actual vignettes' edit and rhythm were not inspected; do not prescribe their timing. |
| BUCK, Square Restaurant Anthem | Documentary interviews and real restaurants informed the story; analogue craft supported the production. | Find an authentic behaviour or supplied observation before decorating a service story. | Synthetic actors are not real testimonials; a handmade look does not prove authenticity. |
| Anna Mantzaris, Apple Fuzzy Feelings | A film combining live action and stop motion around creativity and empathy. | Two visual modes can serve a change in perspective if their relationship is meaningful. | A long-form emotional construction is not automatically suitable for a 25-second product reel. |

These are production accounts, not comparative effectiveness studies. Google’s ABCD guidance concerns YouTube advertising objectives; apply its attention, brand relevance, connection and direction questions only where the task warrants them. Never force a CTA, high brightness, voice-over or a fixed first-second formula into an unrelated brief.

## Eight original decision exercises

These exercises are Studio synthesis, not extracted shots or guaranteed model recipes. Use one or two relevant exercises privately; do not enumerate the library to every user.

| Exercise | Decision to make | Original example | Failure to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Material consequence | What visible change follows an actual interaction with this material? | A brush lifts from a swatch and leaves a ridge; the next angle reveals its surface under a moving highlight. | Inventing drying speed, coverage or product chemistry. |
| One gesture, changed context | Which gesture can connect different situations while preserving its phase? | A hand closes a compact; a cut carries the same descending hand into placing it in a bag. | Repeating the whole action twice or losing the product offscreen. |
| Withheld information | What small missing fact can a later view resolve? | A narrow red shape is revealed as an ordinary colour swatch when the camera widens. | Showing the answer at the start or making the reveal depend on impossible geometry. |
| Functional friction | What credible small obstacle makes the product's role understandable? | Someone clears one place at a crowded craft table before setting down a kit. | Manufacturing a testimonial, a guaranteed benefit or humiliating the user. |
| Stable object, changing reading | Which change around an unchanged product alters how we perceive it? | A controlled light pass reveals a bottle's existing curvature while its label and silhouette stay fixed. | Changing the product itself or claiming a colour shift is the true shade. |
| Consequence outside the frame | Can a sound or reaction suggest an event without adding visual clutter? | A soft lid click precedes a hand relaxing beside the closed container. | Sound inventing an unsupported feature or implying an event that never resolves. |
| Repetition with one meaningful break | What rule can be established quickly, and what exception carries the message? | Three equal layout spaces hold two tools and one finished result. | Repetition that uses time without increasing anticipation or understanding. |
| Deliberate ordinary behaviour | What recognisable action is more precise than a prestige cliché? | A person rotates their hand near a window to inspect the manicure from another angle. | Fake candid behaviour, stock reactions, compulsory beauty smoothing or invented personal experience. |

## Weak-to-specific repairs

### Product reel

**Weak:** a bottle floats through satin, particles and gold light. No event explains the product's role.

**Specific repair:** keep the bottle supported and unchanged. Build a brief sequence around the hand checking a finished colour: rotate the hand, follow its movement toward the bottle, let the last movement reveal the label. Select the light and surface because they make that colour readable. This is a proposed construction, not a claim about an inspected reference or the product's performance.

If the user explicitly wants surreal luxury imagery, floating or satin can be appropriate. The defect is lack of a meaningful relationship, not the presence of a particular prop. Do not turn this example into a permanent ban on satin, particles or surrealism.

### Service story

**Weak:** two people smile, shake hands and instantly solve an unspecified problem.

**Specific repair:** show one credible behaviour changing: the second person stops interrupting and lets the first finish. Give the shot a clear listening action. The service's role and any outcome claim must come from the brief. A fictional scene stays fictional.

### Static communication

**Weak:** add unrelated ornaments and a long slogan to make a poster more creative.

**Specific repair:** identify one visual relationship that communicates the event; give the exact title a clear hierarchy and let supporting shapes reinforce that relationship. Static Graphic Design Creator decides typography, composition and feasibility. Do not force motion terminology or another full copy intake into its work.

### Sound and image

**Weak:** place a generic rise and impact at every transition.

**Specific repair:** choose one sound event that belongs to the action, then decide where silence or continuation helps attention. Audio Production Director owns the cue and music brief. A paper plan does not establish BPM, sync accuracy or commercial music rights.

## Selection and user feedback

Before presenting a direction, answer internally: what happens, why this product or story needs it, what changes, what the viewer can follow, and what would make it fail. Compare alternatives by underlying event, not by three colour palettes for one event. Use the user's requested count; otherwise two or three brief directions are usually enough and four is the ceiling.

Keep Quick delivery to a title and one or two concrete sentences per idea. Preserve necessary uncertainty without dumping worksheets. Expand only the chosen route unless the user requests a complete pack. After an unexplained rejection ask one directional question; do not guess a permanent taste rule or deliver another batch immediately. If the correction already explains the direction, apply it without asking again.

Review with named findings: product relevance, causal clarity, distinctness from nearby alternatives, visual feasibility and fit to this user/project. Use pass, repair or unresolved for specific criteria. Do not invent a numeric originality score, a predicted scroll-stop rate or a claim of unprecedented originality.

## Adding a real project

Use the [decision card](../assets/creative-decision-card.json) only when useful. Record the supplied brief, proposal, exact revision, user's actual decision and stated reason. Mark an assistant hypothesis as such. Keep approved client material in its authorised project store, not in this shared plugin. Add a public teaching case only from a suitable public source or an explicitly permitted, redacted project. Keep failed ideas with their cause when that helps future decisions; do not relabel them as successes.
