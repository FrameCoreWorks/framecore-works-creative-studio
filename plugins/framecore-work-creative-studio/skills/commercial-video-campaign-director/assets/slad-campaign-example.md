# Ślad: a campaign about careful assessment

Original hypothetical teaching direction. Uses the fictional [Ślad scene](../../screenplay-story-architect/assets/slad-scene-example.md). No real customer story, measured service result or brand asset exists.

## Example request and truth

Assume the hypothetical client asks for a short film direction and a related poster concept. Supplied example truth: volunteers inspect small objects and assess repairability. Success is not guaranteed. No address, price, event date, testimonial or measurable advantage is supplied. The requested impression is attention to the object, not instant restoration.

## Direction

**Premise:** the moment an object is received can communicate care before any outcome is known.

**Motion device:** the visitor's hand initially holds the lamp. The volunteer places a felt pad beneath it; the visitor then lets go. The decisive change is in the interaction. The lamp remains unplugged and unrepaired. Keep the receiving gesture readable rather than replacing it with a dramatic repair montage.

**Sound function:** retain the quiet contact and cloth movement if actually recorded or later authorized for production. No triumphant power-on sound. Spoken dialogue stays exactly as in the scene. These are sound intentions, not an existing soundtrack or measured cues.
