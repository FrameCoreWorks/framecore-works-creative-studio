No third-party media was downloaded, replayed, timed or licensed in this research.

## Source selection

Start with the actual maker's project page, a museum/project record where relevant, original research or official technical documentation. Prefer named authors and concrete evidence over generic inspiration lists. Search results are leads; read the destination before promoting a claim. Use source exclusions supplied by the user. Random Facebook/TikTok posts, reposts, anonymous compilations and social engagement counts do not establish a creative method, a product fact or a tool capability. Social platforms are not a required search lane. Where an exact creator account is the only primary source, identify that limitation and respect any user exclusion; do not silently substitute a fan account.

## Production cases

### C01: ManvsMachine, Nike Air Max Day '16

Source: [maker's project page](https://mvsm.com/project/air-max-day-16). Read the project description and available credits/context. The maker describes designers' ideas through kinetic sculptures. Studio inference: select a movement mechanism because it expresses the intended idea, then decide its appearance. No shot timing, optics, effectiveness or physical-product claim was verified. Do not reconstruct the campaign from this text.

### C02: BUCK, A Tiffany Holiday

Source: [maker's project page](https://www.buck.co/work/a-tiffany-holiday). The page describes a hero film and shorter pieces linked by the Bird on the Rock brooch. Studio inference: an asset family can share a distinctive object while individual pieces have different events. Exact edit structures and commercial results remain Unknown. Do not reuse the brooch design or frames as generic client assets.

### C03: BUCK, Square Restaurant Anthem

Source: [maker's production account](https://www.buck.co/work/square-restaurant-anthem). The account describes documentary interviews, real restaurants and analogue production techniques. Studio inference: build a story from supported behaviours and make material treatment serve that choice. Analogue texture is not itself proof of authenticity. Generated spokespeople cannot inherit the status of real interviewed people.

### C04: Anna Mantzaris, Apple Fuzzy Feelings

Source: [director's portfolio](https://www.annamantzaris.se/apple). The page describes an empathy/creativity film combining live action and stop motion, with production credits. Studio inference: a change of visual mode can support a change of perspective. Only the written account was inspected. Do not infer the film's beat map or transfer a long-form story's timing to a short reel.

## Craft and objective guidance

### C05: Adobe, match cuts

Source: [Adobe match-cut guide](https://www.adobe.com/uk/creativecloud/video/discover/match-cut.html). Supports distinguishing visual, action and audio relationships and planning transitions before editing. Studio applies that distinction to pairwise shot review. Film-example details on this explanatory page were not independently checked and are not used as canonical case evidence. No universal rule requires a match cut.

### C06: Adobe, sequence shots

Source: [Adobe sequence-shot guide](https://www.adobe.com/th_en/creativecloud/video/production/cinematography/camera-shots-and-angles/sequence-shot.html). Supports action-phase continuity and awareness of a scene's action axis. Studio inference: inspect entry/exit states and screen direction, while permitting a motivated discontinuity. The source does not validate any generator's ability to reproduce these instructions.

### C07: Google, ABCDs

Source: [Google Ads guidance](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/14783551?hl=en-GB). Describes Attention, Branding, Connection and Direction in the context of YouTube video advertising and its objectives. Use as an objective-sensitive checklist, not a compulsory format for all artwork. No numerical performance uplift is imported into this plugin; no recommendation becomes a guarantee.

## Preference and workflow design

### C08: Radlinski et al., transparent language profiles

Source: [research paper record and abstract](https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.09403), 2022. Proposes natural-language representations as a route to understanding, control and portability of preferences. This update uses the abstract-level argument only; it does not claim an implemented or benchmarked preference-learning algorithm. Studio's explicit scope/evidence records are its own design.

### C09: Nielsen Norman Group, customization and personalization

Source: [Amy Schade's explanation](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/customization-personalization/). Distinguishes adjustments selected by a user from adaptations made by a system. Studio separates an explicit taste instruction from an inferred hypothesis and lets the user inspect or correct either. This is a design application, not evidence about this plugin's accuracy.

### C10: Nielsen Norman Group, customization controls

Source: [Seven tips for customization](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/customization/). Relevant guidance: avoid obstructive initial setup and let users revise earlier choices. Studio applies this to optional profiles and preference retirement. No demographic or personality profiling follows from this source.

### C11: Anthropic, skills architecture

Source: [Agent Skills engineering article](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/equipping-agents-for-the-real-world-with-agent-skills). Describes loading skill resources as needed and improving them from representative use. Studio uses conditional reading and a compact handoff. This article does not document ChatGPT plugin discovery; the dev.30 duplicate finding came from the actual host skill list.

### C12: OpenAI, evaluation design

Source: [evaluation best practices](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/evaluation-best-practices). Supports task-specific evaluations and calibration against human feedback. Studio's pilot records actual selections, defects and repairs; synthetic checks are labelled separately. No evaluation API is required or activated by this integration.

## Access and limits

All twelve sources were retrieved on 2026-09-28. Production pages were read as written accounts. The research abstract was not a full-paper review. An attempted FutureDeluxe project page was inaccessible and supplied no rule. Social search results and the earlier LinkedIn production description were excluded from the final source set after the user's source-quality correction. Source quality is assessed by authorship and evidence, not by a site's visual polish.

Recheck mutable platform/model facts for the actual requested operation. For a new creative brief, use these cases to ask a sharper question, not as a replacement for task-relevant research or actual reference inspection. A useful source must change a decision, establish a limit or define an observable check; otherwise omit it.
