# Polish and EU marketing rules snapshot

Snapshot date: 2026-10-10.

Rules that change what a Polish or EU advertisement, post, label or landing page may say or must show. This is a checklist for the claim ledger's compliance column, not legal advice: Studio flags the rule, writes compliant wording where it can and says what is still open; the user or their counsel decides. Rows marked *general knowledge* were not re-read on the snapshot date.

## How to use it

When copy or a graphic contains one of the triggers below, add the rule to the [claim ledger](../../ecommerce-campaign-strategy-director/templates/ecommerce-campaign-strategy-pack.md#copy-and-claim-ledger) row as `flag`, `compliant wording drafted` or `needs counsel`, with the snapshot date. Within 90 days of the snapshot date it satisfies the research trigger for these rules in Quick mode; a status marked *changing* needs a fresh check before a final.

## Rules

| Trigger in the work | Rule | What the work needs | Status on 2026-10-10 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A price reduction: "−20 %", "promocja", "taniej o 50 zł", a crossed-out price | Price information act, art. 4 ust. 2 (Omnibus), from 1 January 2023; UOKiK explanations of 8 May 2023 | Next to the reduced price, the lowest price of the last 30 days before the reduction, legible; the percentage or amount counted from that price | In force; UOKiK monitors it. The compositor example sets this line next to the price |
| Any price to consumers | Price information act | The gross price (with VAT) in złoty; a unit price where the act requires it | In force (*general knowledge* for the unit-price detail) |
| A post or video paid with money, a discount or a free product | UOKiK recommendations on labelling advertising by influencers, 26 September 2022 (unfair market practices act) | A clear label at the start: `#reklama`, `[REKLAMA]`, `#współpracareklamowa`, "Płatna współpraca z marką X"; `#współpraca` alone is not enough; also for self-promotion of one's own products | In force; the influencer, the agency and the advertiser are responsible |
| A newsletter, SMS or phone offer, a sign-up form | Electronic communications law (PKE), art. 398, in force since 10 November 2024; GDPR for the data | Prior consent per channel, given actively (an unticked box), separate from other consents and easy to withdraw; the form says who sends what | In force |
| A realistic generated image, voice or video that could pass as real: a real person, place, event or a product shown as photographed | AI Act (Regulation 2024/1689), art. 50, applies from 2 August 2026 | A clear, visible statement that the content is generated or altered; for evidently artistic, satirical or fictional work a lighter disclosure that does not spoil it. AI-generated text on matters of public interest needs disclosure unless a person reviewed it and carries editorial responsibility | Applies; Commission guidelines and a code of practice were in draft or new on the snapshot date. Platforms add their own AI labels |
| "Eko", "zielony", "przyjazny dla środowiska", "neutralny klimatycznie", a sustainability badge | Directive 2024/825 (empowering consumers for the green transition), applies from 27 September 2026 | No generic environmental claim without recognised excellent environmental performance; no climate-neutral claim based on offsetting; sustainability labels only from certification schemes or public authorities; durability and repair claims must be true | *Changing*: Poland had not notified full transposition (infringement procedure opened 28 May 2026); the directive's dates are the safe assumption |
| A health or nutrition claim on food or a supplement: "wzmacnia odporność", "źródło białka" | Regulation (EC) 1924/2006 | Only claims authorised in the EU register, with their conditions of use; a supplement may not claim to prevent or cure a disease | In force (*general knowledge*) |
| An over-the-counter medicine | Pharmaceutical law | The mandatory warning about the leaflet and the restrictions on medicine advertising | In force (*general knowledge*); always counsel |
| Beer or any alcohol | Act on upbringing in sobriety, art. 13¹ | Only beer may be advertised, under conditions (not aimed at or showing minors, no link to success, health or driving, a health warning taking a set share of the ad); other alcohol may not be advertised | *Changing*: a bill removing the beer exception went to second reading on 29 July 2026 with a vote announced for September; the outcome was not found. Check before any alcohol work |
| Consumer credit, instalments, "0 %" | Consumer credit act, art. 7 and 8 | An ad that states a cost figure gives the annual percentage rate (RRSO) and the other required figures on a representative example | In force; an amendment on credit advertising was reported for 20 November 2026 (status not confirmed). Always counsel |
| Cosmetics claims | Regulation (EU) 655/2013, common criteria | Claims that are legally compliant, true, supported by evidence, honest and fair | In force (*general knowledge*) |
| "Najlepszy", "nr 1", "najtańszy", a comparison with a named competitor | Unfair competition act, art. 16; unfair market practices act | Evidence the user holds for a superlative; a comparison that is objective, verifiable and fair | In force (*general knowledge*). A search is not substantiation |
| Offers, descriptions and warnings for consumers in Poland | Polish language act | The Polish version of consumer-facing descriptions, warnings and terms; brand names and slogans may stay in another language | In force (*general knowledge*) |
