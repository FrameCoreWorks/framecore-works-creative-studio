# Routing boundaries and special cases

Read when a request sits between two owners, mixes modalities, supplies a conversation link, or asks about an unsupported module or reasoning setting. These rules extend the route table in [the entry](../SKILL.md); they do not add owners.

## Collisions

Settled defaults for requests two owners could take. A stated preference, a named tool or an existing project decides first.

- **Image prompt.** A designed piece (poster, flyer, ad, social graphic, cover, packaging, logo) goes to Static Graphic Design Creator even when only its prompt is wanted, because layout, hierarchy and exact copy are design decisions. A prompt for an image that is not a layout (a product photo, an illustration, a scene) or for an already approved direction goes to Image Prompt Architect.
- **Product video.** An idea or a family of films goes to Commercial Video Campaign Director; the whole production across script, shots, prompts, sound, captions and edit to Creative Video Producer; a prompt for a named generator to Video Prompt Architect; the sales strategy behind the ads to Ecommerce Campaign Strategy Director. A film from the user's product photos, screenshots or logo with no generator named gets the [short choice](#generated-video-or-motion-from-code): Motion Graphics Workflow for a coded MP4, or the [end-to-end route](product-film-end-to-end-route.md) for generated video.
- **Supplied video.** A generated clip to judge or fix goes to Video Prompt Architect (review and a repair prompt); a video Studio rendered from code to Motion Graphics Workflow and its review tools; footage/highlights/full or mixed-media editing and FrameCut revisions to FrameCut (`opencut-video-studio`); its captions to Caption Studio; its sound design or mixing to Motion Graphics Workflow when code execution is available, otherwise to Audio Production Director for a production plan. Standalone audio review, lyrics and music briefs stay with Audio Production Director. Output Critic Iteration reviews still images only; a frame or thumbnail does not establish motion quality.
- **Service businesses.** A clinic, salon, studio, office or contractor without a priced offer to sell (awareness, positioning, a channel plan) goes to Marketing. A priced offer, package, promotion or booking campaign goes to Ecommerce Campaign Strategy Director, as a product would; Marketing supplies missing brand foundations.
- **Copy, lyrics and dialogue.** Commercial wording goes to Copy Voice, polish of existing text to Humanizer, lyrics and jingles to Audio Production Director, narrative dialogue and ad scripts to Screenplay Story Architect, creator reads to UGC with Copy Voice.
- **Pages, mockups and decks.** A landing page, website or app screen mockup, an HTML page or the design of a slide or pitch deck goes to Static Graphic Design Creator with the [craft rules](../../pipeline-core/references/craft-rules.md) and `craft_lint.py`; its words come from Copy Voice when they are a separate deliverable, the export from Delivery Documentation. A lesson presentation stays in the teacher profile. A UI or landing shown inside a film stays with Motion Graphics Workflow.
- **Contrast and accessibility.** A supplied still is checked by Output Critic, which measures the colour pair with `contrast_check.py` where code runs; HTML, CSS or SVG by its owner with `craft_lint.py` ([craft lint](../../pipeline-core/assets/craft-lint/README.md)).
- **Reference animation.** Measuring a supplied reference animation (cuts, beat grid, transitions, what moves) to rebuild its technique goes to Motion Graphics Workflow with [reference breakdown](../../hyperframes-workflow/assets/reference-breakdown/README.md); a still reference to Reference Pack Curator.
- **Enlarging an image.** An image too small for print or a placement goes to Static Graphic Design Creator's [upscaling](../../static-graphic-design-creator/references/upscaling.md) route: measure with `image_probe.py`, prefer a better source, enlarge locally (Upscayl is optional), with a model licensed for paid work.
- **Songs on screen.** A coded lyric video, beat-synced typography or an animation driven by the track goes to Motion Graphics Workflow ([lyric timing](../../hyperframes-workflow/assets/motion-sync/README.md#lyric-timing), `audio_envelope.py`); a music video with performance or a story to Creative Music Video Director, which hands a coded section back to Motion Graphics.

## Boundaries

The sequence and board owners are distinct: the first structures events and shot cards over time; the second designs a static comparison/layout artifact with exact visible labels. The board is not automatically a video frame or continuity carrier. Keep storyboard timing, action, camera, light, palette, wardrobe, location and continuity legible per panel; distinguish a temporal sequence board from a character/product/look reference sheet. For recurring realistic human identity, request only useful reference views that are missing; do not invent facial structure from one view or beautify away visible traits. An owner is a responsibility, not evidence that another agent ran. Use tools only when the requested operation is exposed and authorized. A small task may be completed directly using the needed reference without another greeting or intake.

Campaign direction, sequence design and prompt compilation are also separate: the campaign owner establishes why the film exists, its motion idea, asset roles and truth locks; the sequence owner structures timed events and shot cards; the video-prompt owner maps a selected shot or edit to a researched generator surface. Do not expand a single-prompt request into campaign strategy or a storyboard request into new direction.

Music-video direction is a separate route from a product/brand campaign. The music-video owner develops the song/persona image logic; it does not silently write the screenplay, shot cards or generator prompt. Resolve a material mixed-goal brief by identifying whether the primary outcome is the artist/song or a commercial campaign.

Keep music-video concept and production packet separate. Route an artist-led concept to `creative-music-video-director`; use `audio-production-director` for music, sound, VO, lyrics, rights research or supplied-audio triage, and preserve approved decisions across multiple stages. An audio-review request does not require a prompt or packet. Narrative scenes, sequence cards and video-model-native prompt syntax remain with their existing owners. Audio Production Director authors text and may coordinate available, authorized inspection; it is not a Flow Music integration. Google Flow Music (formerly ProducerAI) is one provider route whose name, features and terms must be checked against current official sources. Route original narrative dialogue to the screenplay owner, commercial/editorial wording to Copy Voice, with Humanizer for polish, and lyrics to Audio Production Director; compilers preserve selected text rather than rewriting it.

For supplied conversation links, use the active host's available web/open capability to attempt that exact URL when the user asks to resume it. Use recovered conversation context, then name any unavailable attachment or missing decision; do not claim automatic synchronization. If the user asks for a Codex↔ChatGPT Work transfer, create the [cross-host handoff](../assets/cross-host-handoff.template.md) with project context, current decisions, exact locks, sources, asset/attachment availability, tool state, authorization and next action. Include only the preferences needed for the project unless they request their broader profile.

For a complex creative brief, users may choose a Thinking-capable model or deeper reasoning setting when their host exposes one. The Studio cannot set or enforce that host preference through its package, and deeper reasoning is not a guarantee against mistakes; verify the actual artifact against its locks and acceptance criteria.

For an unsupported module, say what is not packaged. You may answer within general host capabilities, but do not attribute that answer to a completed Studio module or invent a missing skill.

For mixed poster/film/music briefs, keep one shared concept and assign each requested artifact to its owner. A campaign can pass its sonic intent to Audio Production Director as a bounded music/voice/sound brief; it does not need a new concept owner. Classify a supplied result by its actual modality before choosing a reviewer. A video thumbnail does not establish temporal quality, and an audio filename does not establish sound quality.

For a product-photo-to-reel project, follow the integrated [end-to-end route](product-film-end-to-end-route.md) while keeping owners and execution states distinct. For multiple owners, resumable projects, tool uncertainty or linked revisions, use [capabilities and handoffs](capabilities-and-handoffs.md). Load only the relevant specialist references; reuse evidence for the unchanged question and refresh the part affected by a new fact, model, source or decision. Keep a short task short.

## Generated video or motion from code

A video request can go two ways: prompts for a video generator that the user runs elsewhere, or motion graphics built from code, which Studio renders here as a finished MP4, with designed sound on request, where code execution exists ([Motion Graphics Workflow](../../hyperframes-workflow/SKILL.md)). When a request names neither and its content could be built from text, logos, screenshots, data, diagrams or product photos on designed backgrounds, offer both in one short choice and say what each delivers; do not ask for a brief first. Footage-like realism (people acting, real places, a camera moving through a scene) stays with the generative route. A product or app film from the user's photos or screenshots fits the code route ([product films](../../hyperframes-workflow/references/product-films.md)). A named generator, a supplied runtime, an existing project or a stated preference decides without a question. In a host without code execution the code route delivers the contract and the motion player, where the user exports the video; say so in the choice.

## FrameCut ownership

FrameCut owns a requested edit and its single revisioned timeline through delivery. Motion Graphics Workflow, Caption Studio and Audio Production Director contribute scoped artifacts at the same revision, without restarting intake. Creative Video Producer retains campaign/preproduction coordination; a pure coded-motion task can stay with its existing motion owner. A generated-clip repair prompt remains with Video Prompt Architect. A direct FrameCut task or resume bypasses welcome; option 5 is interpreted only in the pending five-option welcome. A specialist handoff does not register a second master project.
