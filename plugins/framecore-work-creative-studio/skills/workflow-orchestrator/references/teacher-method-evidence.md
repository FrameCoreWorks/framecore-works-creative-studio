# Teacher method evidence

Snapshot: 2026-10-03. Sources were read for bounded design principles. This file is not a curriculum registry or evidence that Studio's original activities have been tested with pupils.

| Primary source | Bounded use in this profile | Limit |
|---|---|---|
| [CAST Universal Design for Learning Guidelines](https://udlguidelines.cast.org/) | Consider access barriers, clear goals and appropriate options for engagement, representation and response. This informs support choices, not labels assigned to pupils. | The official site identifies Guidelines 3.0, released in July 2024. The profile does not reproduce the framework or claim CAST certification. |
| [IES / What Works Clearinghouse: Organizing Instruction and Study to Improve Student Learning](https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/PracticeGuide/1) | Consider spaced revisiting, worked examples with practice, explanatory questions and retrieval of taught content. Use them when aligned with the objective. | This is a 2007 guide, not new 2026 research. Recommendations have different evidence grades: spacing and worked-example interleaving are moderate; quiz re-exposure and deep explanatory questions are strong; prequestions are minimal. No uniform strength or guaranteed gain is implied. |
| [UNESCO: Guidance for generative AI in education and research](https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/guidance-generative-ai-education-and-research) | Keep human educational judgment, age-appropriate use, privacy and contextual validation visible when preparing AI-assisted materials. | Guidance published in 2023; the article was updated in January 2026. It is not a legal determination or a platform-specific age policy. |

## Evidence boundaries

The original examples and activity bank were independently authored for this package. They are proposals to adapt and review, with fictional scenarios or explicitly synthetic data. A source supporting a general teaching principle does not validate a particular lesson, reading level, duration or measurable outcome.

No current national curriculum provision was mapped in this release. For a requested mapping, Research Evidence must inspect the relevant current official document and record jurisdiction, version, requirement locator, task and check. Recheck changing policy, qualification, platform or legal claims when needed.

The delivered base contains no third-party commercial lesson files, copied catalogue, branded exercise names or links to the business/profile used for initial needs discovery. Public availability does not establish reuse rights or effectiveness.
