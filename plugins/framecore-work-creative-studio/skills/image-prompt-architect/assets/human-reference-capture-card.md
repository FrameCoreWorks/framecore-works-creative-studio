# Photoreal human reference capture card

Use only when continuity or likeness of a real or recurring photoreal person materially matters. Request only the views that solve the task. Do not turn a single portrait prompt into a burdensome intake.

## Useful views when identity is important

- A sharp, neutrally lit front view without beauty filters or strong perspective distortion.
- Left and right three-quarter views; add true profiles only if the planned camera sees them.
- A natural expression reference and the specific expressions required by the scene. Avoid a wall of arbitrary emotions.
- One wider view for height, posture, body proportions and clothing when full-body shots are planned.
- Extra high/low camera angles, hair/back views, glasses, jewelry, tattoos or garment construction only when they will appear.

Ask for the missing useful view in one concise question at a time. Say why a particular angle matters. If only one image exists, anchor to visible traits and mark occluded details as unknown; do not invent exact anatomy, skin tone at another light, hairstyle behind the head, clothing seams or other unseen facts.

## Preserve believable skin and people

For photoreal work, describe the real person's visible, distinguishing features and photographic conditions. Preserve natural pores, fine lines, asymmetry and ordinary tonal variation when visible. Do not add face slimming, age reduction, makeup, skin smoothing, artificial gloss, fashion retouch or an idealized replacement identity unless explicitly requested. Keep a requested illustration, stylized character or 3D design in its chosen medium instead of forcing photorealism.

## Identity sheet and review

Bind source images by alias and view; preserve which properties each controls. A generated multi-panel sheet is a candidate layout, not proof every panel matches. Review representative views against the supplied source for face shape, hair, eyes, marks, build and wardrobe continuity. Mark panels as verified, candidate, mismatched or not inspected. Use exact labels and readable separation. Search current model guidance for the actual generator and operation; never promise perfect identity from a prompt or a single photo.
