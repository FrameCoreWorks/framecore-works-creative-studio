# Worked design: one half-turn mechanism in two formats

Original fictional Studio teaching example, 2026-09-28. No real exhibition,
commission, image, font file or measured test is asserted. The hypothetical
user has selected the mechanism and exact text below and requests two
standalone image prompts, not generation. The typography is part of each
integrated raster; no later overlay is assumed.

## Accepted brief for this example

- Purpose: announce an exhibition about composition.
- Mechanism: two opposed semicircular paper planes make a half-turn visible;
  a narrow straight gap separates them. The title stays upright and intact.
- Palette/material: warm cream field, one muted ochre plane, one charcoal plane;
  restrained paper edges and slight relief, not generic distressed texture.
- Locked: meaning, copy, character-level spelling, semantic reading order,
  the two-plane relation and upright lettering.
- Allowed: change scale, placement, orientation of the central gap, grouping,
  and line breaks between words in the supporting lines.
- Requested formats: 4:5 portrait and 1:1 square digital graphics.
- Not supplied: official placement safe areas, final pixel dimensions, printer
  requirements or a particular font file. Do not invent them.

## Portrait composition and complete prompt

**Design decision:** keep the title as a stable horizontal entry. Below it,
the opposed planes form a tall central relation around a vertical gap. The
practical block sits in a clear lower territory. The plane edges explain the
composition theme without turning the small information into experimental type.

```text
Create one finished vertical 4:5 digital exhibition poster. The concept is a half-turn: two opposed semicircular paper planes, one muted ochre and one charcoal, face opposite directions across a narrow straight vertical gap. Keep their relationship simple, deliberate and visually readable. Use a warm cream field, restrained paper-edge detail and slight consistent relief. The page is flat poster artwork, not a photograph of a poster or a perspective mockup.

Place the title prominently across the upper field in upright, moderately wide uppercase sans-serif lettering. Keep its word intact on one line with clear counters, natural spacing and undistorted Polish characters. The opposed paper planes occupy the central field below, with sufficient separation to remain legible as two parts of one half-turn mechanism. Let their straight gap guide attention toward the practical information without crossing letters.

Use exactly these four visible strings, once each, in this reading order:
"PÓŁOBRÓT"
"WYSTAWA KOMPOZYCJI"
"10–24 PAŹDZIERNIKA"
"GALERIA RÓWNOLEGŁA"

Place the second line immediately below the title at a quieter scale. Keep the date and venue in a clear, aligned lower information block. Preserve every character, including Ó, Ł, Ź and the en dash in the date. Supporting lines may wrap between words when necessary, with no wording or punctuation changes. Maintain strong local separation between charcoal type and the warm cream field, enough interior breathing space for all required copy, and no paper texture across the letterforms.

The title is the first read, the half-turn relation the visual discovery, and the date and venue remain easy to retrieve. Keep all lettering upright; rotation belongs only to the paper-plane relationship. Add no other visible text, marks, logos, credits or decorative microcopy. Return one coherent integrated poster in which image and text are designed together.
```

## Square adaptation and complete prompt

**What changes:** the gap becomes horizontal so the opposed planes form a low,
broad relation. The title/support and the practical block use distinct left
alignment edges around that relation. **What stays:** the half-turn argument,
paper logic, exact four strings, upright title and reading order. This is a
recomposition, not evidence of exported or cropped files.

```text
Create one finished square 1:1 digital exhibition graphic. Express a half-turn through two opposed semicircular paper planes, one muted ochre and one charcoal, facing opposite directions across a narrow straight horizontal gap. Arrange the pair as a low, broad central relationship with restrained paper-edge detail and slight consistent relief against a warm cream field. Keep the graphic flat and immediately usable as artwork, not a mockup or a board of variations.

Make the title the first read in the upper-left territory, set on one line in upright, moderately wide uppercase sans-serif lettering with clear counters and undistorted Polish characters. Place the supporting exhibition line directly beneath it. Keep the low paper-plane pair below this entry and allow its horizontal gap to lead toward a distinct, aligned date-and-venue block in the lower-right interior. The shape relation must remain readable without covering any required letters.

The entire visible text inventory is exactly these four strings, once each, in this reading order:
"PÓŁOBRÓT"
"WYSTAWA KOMPOZYCJI"
"10–24 PAŹDZIERNIKA"
"GALERIA RÓWNOLEGŁA"

Preserve all spelling, capitalization, Polish characters and punctuation, including the en dash in the date. Keep the title intact on one line. Supporting lines may wrap only between words, retaining the complete wording. Use charcoal type on clear warm cream territories with comfortable space from the image shapes and crop. The practical details are subordinate in scale but fully readable at the intended use size; they are not decorative fine print.

Keep lettering upright and the two-plane half-turn mechanism recognizable. Add no new slogan, logo, address, year, credits, symbols with factual meaning or extra visible wording. Keep texture off the text and avoid distressed effects. Deliver one complete integrated square graphic with a deliberate title-to-image-to-information path.
```

## Planned review and a repair fork

1. Read C1–C4 character-by-character in each actual output; inspect accents and
   the date separator rather than trusting the overall word shapes.
2. Check the complete half-turn relation in both formats; palette alone is not
   sufficient continuity.
3. At the intended use size, test that C3 and C4 remain readable and correctly
   paired. At reduced size, check that C1 still leads.
4. If the square's planes dominate the title, adjust their permitted scale or
   spacing. Do not add a new headline or remove the venue to regain balance.
5. If a required glyph is wrong but the design works, use the existing scoped
   repair route, then recheck all copy; do not redesign the concept by default.
