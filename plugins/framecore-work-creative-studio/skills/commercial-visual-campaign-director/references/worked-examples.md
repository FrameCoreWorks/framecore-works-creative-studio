# Worked static-design examples

Owner: commercial-visual-campaign-director. Version: 0.1.0-dev.1. Original synthetic teaching examples, 2026-09-21. None reports a generated image, measured audience response or successful host run. The briefs and names are fictional. Adapt the method, not the recurring subject.

## Example 1: complete directed poster prompt

User brief: “Potrzebuję tylko promptu. Plakat cyfrowy 4:5 dla fikcyjnej wystawy. Zatwierdzony tekst: PRÓG / wystawa o tym, co pomiędzy / 18–30 października / Galeria Przejście. Chcę, aby jedna wąska szczelina światła była granicą między dwoma polami. Bez fotografii, logotypu i dodatkowych napisów. Prompt po angielsku, polski tekst bez zmian.”

The user already selected direction, text, medium and language. Do not greet again, offer three routes, change the title or ask whether the strings are approved. Since the user asked only for a prompt, no generation and no consent footer.

~~~text
Create one finished flat digital exhibition poster in a vertical 4:5 composition. The visual thesis is a threshold: a very narrow vertical slit of light separates two broad, quiet color fields. The title is the first read, the light boundary the second visual discovery, followed by the exhibition details.

Use a warm off-white left field and a deep muted charcoal right field. Place the light slit slightly right of the center axis. It is a clean, restrained boundary, not a glow cloud or a depiction of a doorway. Keep the artwork graphic and planar, without photography, perspective mockup or decorative objects.

Set the title in large, compact uppercase sans-serif lettering within the upper-left field, with clear counters and deliberate spacing. Let the right edge of the title block approach the slit without crossing it. Place the supporting sentence below with enough separation to establish a quieter reading level. Align the date and venue in a small but clearly readable lower-left information block.

Render exactly these four Polish strings, once each:
Title: "PRÓG"
Support: "wystawa o tym, co pomiędzy"
Date: "18–30 października"
Venue: "Galeria Przejście"

Preserve every accent, case and punctuation mark. Keep each string on a single line. Protect comfortable space between the information block and the crop. The charcoal field remains largely empty so that the threshold relation carries the idea.

Produce the poster artwork itself, not a photograph of it, a moodboard or a grid of variations. Add no other visible text, logo or mark. The finish is precise: coherent optical alignment, crisp type, strong local contrast, and no texture crossing the letterforms. The result must preserve the narrow light-boundary mechanism and readable exact event information.
~~~

Checks after any future render: all four strings exact; title first; no doorway/room added; slit remains narrow; practical text readable at intended display size. These are planned checks, not a passed render review.

## Example 2: deep exploration without premature copy lock

User brief: “Robimy fikcyjny sąsiedzki wieczór napraw. Ludzie przynoszą jedną małą rzecz, wolontariusze oceniają możliwość naprawy. Chcę trzy różne kierunki plakatu. Nie mamy jeszcze hasła ani daty.”

Do not invent date, price, guaranteed repair or a final prompt. Provide actual paired proposals:

### A. Jeszcze jeden wieczór

Proposed headline: “Jeszcze nie koniec.”

Visual mechanism: one ordinary table lamp with an obviously repaired, carefully wrapped joint; the electrical construction is not shown as a safe technical instruction. The light falls only across a small usable area of a table. The repaired object, not tools, is the protagonist.

Supporting proposal: “Przynieś jedną małą rzecz. Sprawdzimy, czy da się ją naprawić.”

Composition: lamp and its modest light occupy the lower-right; the headline sits in the calm upper-left; event details get a separate factual block once supplied.

Why: emotional value appears through continued ordinary use. Tradeoff: lamp-specific imagery needs a line that makes the wider event scope clear.

### B. Miejsce przy stole

Proposed headline: “Zróbmy jej miejsce.”

Visual mechanism: a top-down worktable with one carefully reserved empty space between two active working areas. The absence is an invitation for the visitor's object, not a random minimalist gap.

Supporting proposal: “Sąsiedzki wieczór napraw. Jedna mała rzecz na początek.”

Composition: the reserved rectangle interrupts the table rhythm and aligns with the headline block. Practical information belongs to a clean perimeter band, not scribbled tool labels.

Why: participation is the main entry point. Tradeoff: the metaphor is less immediate and depends on clear support.

### C. Sprawdź, zanim wyrzucisz

Proposed headline: “Najpierw sprawdźmy.”

Visual mechanism: an object outlined by an inspection gesture, with a simple open-ended test mark rather than a success tick. The design emphasizes assessment and honest uncertainty.

Supporting proposal: “Przynieś jedną małą rzecz do oceny możliwości naprawy.”

Composition: blunt large headline; a single object/inspection relationship below; direct factual type system. No invented certification or percentage.

Why: most direct communication of what the event actually promises. Tradeoff: less emotionally indirect than A.

A useful closing decision: recommend the route based on the user's priority, then ask which mechanism and exact wording to develop. Mark all copy as proposed. Dates/venue remain missing, not printed placeholders in a “final” artifact.

## Example 3: campaign adaptation with invariant meaning

Approved fictional master: “Mniej hałasu. Więcej miejsca.”, support “Wieczór spokojnej pracy”, date “24 października, 18:00”, venue “Pracownia Północ”. Mechanism: a dense field of short neutral strokes ends at a clean edge, making room for the title. The strokes are abstract, not words or data. No product or logo.

Two requested outputs: 9:16 digital story and 3:1 horizontal banner. This is a layout adaptation, not permission to shorten copy. Safe-area values are not supplied, so the prompts use protected interior territories without claiming official platform coordinates.

### 9:16 prompt

~~~text
Create one flat vertical 9:16 digital event graphic. The concept is room for attention: a dense, controlled field of short dark abstract strokes occupies the upper quarter and stops at a precise horizontal boundary. Below it, a broad quiet warm-white field holds the message. The strokes contain no letters, numbers or symbols with factual meaning.

Place the main statement prominently in the upper part of the quiet field using a clear, medium-wide sans-serif. Give its two sentences separate lines, retaining their punctuation. Place the support below in a quieter weight. Group date and venue together in the lower-middle interior, leaving generous breathing space above the bottom crop and below the top edge.

The entire visible text inventory is:
"Mniej hałasu. Więcej miejsca."
"Wieczór spokojnej pracy"
"24 października, 18:00"
"Pracownia Północ"

Preserve all Polish characters and exact wording. Use near-black type against the warm-white field. The title leads; the abstract strokes establish the contrast without competing; date and venue are easy to retrieve. No logos, fake microcopy or added CTA. This is the finished artwork, not a phone mockup. Keep required text well inside the canvas; final platform overlay safe areas require a separate check.
~~~

### 3:1 prompt

~~~text
Create one flat horizontal 3:1 digital event banner. Preserve the concept of room for attention: a dense field of short dark abstract strokes fills the leftmost portion and stops at a crisp vertical edge. The remaining broad warm-white field is quiet. The strokes are purely abstract and do not form text, numbers or informative symbols.

Use a lateral reading sequence. Place the main statement in large clear sans-serif lettering immediately to the right of the stopping edge. Put its two sentences on separate lines. Arrange the support, date and venue as a readable compact block farther right, aligned consistently and clearly separated from the headline.

Render only these exact Polish strings, once each:
"Mniej hałasu. Więcej miejsca."
"Wieczór spokojnej pracy"
"24 października, 18:00"
"Pracownia Północ"

Keep the main statement dominant while preserving the practical block at a comfortable reading size. Maintain near-black on warm-white, generous outer breathing room and precise spacing. Do not crop any required information. No logo, extra CTA, invented text, perspective mockup or grid of variants. The abstract-to-quiet boundary must remain the recognizable campaign device.
~~~

What changed: boundary orientation and information grouping. What stayed: exact copy, contrast mechanism, type behavior and palette relation. Acceptance requires actual inspection of both outputs; matching colors alone is not enough.

## Example 4: one local correction

Scenario: the user supplied an actual poster and requests replacing only “18 października” with “19 października”. The image must really be available to the edit operation. This example does not claim to inspect such a file.

~~~text
Edit the supplied poster only to replace the visible date "18 października" with "19 października". Keep the date in its current position, hierarchy, type appearance, color and alignment.

Preserve every other visible string exactly, as well as the approved title, artwork, crop, palette, spacing, logo and all image relationships. Do not redesign the composition, add new wording or change any other factual information.

Return one revised poster. The target check is the exact new date with all Polish characters intact; the preservation check is that every other text item and the approved layout remain unchanged.
~~~

If the source image is absent, ask for it instead of pretending this prompt is a completed edit. If the tool fails, report failure. If the new date is right but another word drifts, reject that result.

## Example 5: production master, not raster theater

User brief: “Mam zaakceptowany projekt. Potrzebuję menu do drukarni z 48 pozycjami, dokładnym fontem, cenami, alergenami i plikiem edytowalnym.”

Appropriate response is a production handoff, not another generated raster:

- Collect the approved item/price/allergen table, exact copy and actual font/license assets.
- Confirm the real trim size, page/panel count, printer requirements and editable-file target.
- Preserve item-to-price and item-to-allergen relationships; no inferred ingredients.
- Define repeatable category and row structure with sufficient reading space.
- Record unknown profile, bleed and finishing requirements for the printer.
- Require a content proof and actual editable/print export verification.

Do not claim this list is a completed menu file. Do not promise a generated QR or remove rows to fit. A separate illustrative food image can be a useful asset if requested, but it does not satisfy the editable menu contract.

## Example 6: target-aware wording without false controls

Suppose the user names FLUX.2 pro and requests a prompt only. The current official guide states that negative prompts are not supported in its documented scope. Preserve the creative contract while describing the desired final state positively.

Use an exclusive visible-copy inventory, intended empty regions and crisp field relationships in the main prompt. Do not append a fabricated negative_prompt API field, CFG value or Midjourney-style flag. If the user has not raised an API or wrapper, do not ask them to identify one merely to deliver ordinary prose.

A prompt may resemble the portable example because the creative facts are unchanged. Its correctness depends on the mapped contract, not on an arbitrary amount of different wording.
