# Client emails (English)

Short emails for each step of a client project, the English source version of the [Polish kit](client-emails.pl.md). Fill the braces, keep one tone of address for the whole thread, check the result against the [AI writing patterns](../references/ai-writing-patterns.md), and never promise what the offer does not say. Signatures, terms and prices are the user's. For another language, translate from this version and follow that language's conventions for formality.

## 1. Questions before the offer

Subject: {Project}: a few questions before I quote

Hello {name},
thank you for your message. To prepare an accurate offer I need a little more information. Short answers in a reply or in the attached brief are enough:
1. {the most important open question}
2. {the second}
3. {the third}
I will send the offer within {X} working days of your answers.
{signature}

## 2. Offer

Subject: {Project}: offer

Hello {name},
attached is my offer for {project}: {number} scope options, the schedule and the price. I recommend {package}, because {one sentence about the client's goal}.
The offer is valid until {date}. Once you accept, I will send {the deposit invoice / the contract} and start with {first step}.
I am happy to answer any questions.
{signature}

## 3. Presenting concepts

Subject: {Project}: directions to choose from

Hello {name},
here are {two / three} directions, each shown where it will be used. My recommendation is {direction}, because {reason}.
Please reply with one number (1, 2, 3, or 4 with a description of the change) by {date}, so we keep the {date} deadline.
{signature}

## 4. Summary of feedback before a revision round

Subject: {Project}: feedback on version {r}, please confirm

Hello {name},
I have collected the feedback into one list:
1. {point}
2. {point}
{If points conflict:} Points {x} and {y} rule each other out. Which version shall we go with?
This is {round {n} of {N} / a change of scope, quoted at {amount}}. I will start once you confirm.
{signature}

## 6. Payment reminder

Subject: {Project}: invoice no. {no.}

Hello {name},
a friendly reminder about invoice no. {no.} for {amount}, due on {date}. If the payment is already on its way, please ignore this message.
{signature}

## 7. Asking for a testimonial

Subject: {Project}: a short request

Hello {name},
thank you for the project. May I show it in my portfolio? If so, I would be grateful for two or three sentences about working together. I will only publish them in a form you have approved.
{signature}
