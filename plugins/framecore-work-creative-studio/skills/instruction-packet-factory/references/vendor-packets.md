# Packets for people

A packet for a person outside Studio: a print shop, a voice talent, a freelance illustrator, animator, photographer, editor or translator. It is written in the language the person works in (usually Polish), sent by the user, and is complete enough that the person needs no call to start. Use the [vendor packet template](../assets/vendor-packet.template.md); every price, rate, payment term and legal clause is the user's input, never a default. Studio sends nothing and signs nothing.

## Every packet

- **Job and owner.** Job name, client (only what the vendor needs), the user's contact, the decision maker for questions.
- **What to make.** One sentence of the result, then the deliverables as a numbered list with format, size, quantity and file naming.
- **Locks.** Exact copy (pasted, never retyped), brand kit values, approved references with their role (match, inspire, avoid), what may and may not change.
- **Dates.** Proof or first draft, review rounds and their count, final delivery; the time zone if it matters.
- **Acceptance.** Checks the user will run on return, written so the vendor can run them first.
- **Rights and money.** Usage scope (fields of exploitation, media, territory, term), credit line, confidentiality, AI-use disclosure, price and payment terms: all from the user, marked "to agree" when missing.
- **Return.** What comes back (masters, sources, separated layers or stems) and how.

Leave out other clients, private notes, internal prices and anything the vendor does not need.

## Print shop

Product and quantity; trimmed size; bleed (3 mm unless the shop says otherwise) and safe margin; colour (CMYK with the shop's profile, Pantone if any); paper (type and weight), finish (varnish, lamination, foil), folding or binding; file type the shop wants (usually PDF/X with fonts embedded or outlined), resolution (300 ppi effective at final size); proof type (digital or printed) and who approves it; delivery address and date. Ask the shop for its own file specification and follow it over these defaults. Check against the [format and print presets](../../static-graphic-design-creator/references/format-and-print-presets.md) and the [delivery documentation](../../delivery-documentation/SKILL.md) before sending. On return: count, colour against the approved proof, trim and folding, damage.

## Voice talent

The final script, word for word, with the brand names, foreign words and numbers spelled as said (a pronunciation list); length per version and the word budget from the [short-form timing card](../../pipeline-core/references/short-form-timing.md); pace (calm, standard, fast), tone in two or three words with a reference read if one exists; delivery: WAV, 48 kHz, 24-bit, mono, each take a separate file, 5 seconds of room tone, no processing unless agreed; pickups (how many are included); usage (media, territory, term) and whether the voice may be used for AI training or cloning (never without the talent's explicit written consent). On return: script match word for word, length within half a second of the target, no clipping, steady noise floor.

## Freelancer (illustration, animation, photography, editing, translation)

Scope in and out; deliverables with formats and sizes; the brief, approved direction and references; locks; milestones (sketch, draft, final) with review rounds; acceptance criteria per milestone; usage rights and their transfer, credit, confidentiality; whether AI tools may be used and how their use is disclosed; source files to return (layered files, project files, raw photos, subtitle files). For translation: the source text, target language and register, terms that stay untranslated, length limits per line.
