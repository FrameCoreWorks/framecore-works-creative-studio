# Ślad: an original scene and a bounded revision

Entirely fictional teaching project authored for this package. “Ślad” is a made-up repair workshop, not a real service. Characters, props and dialogue are invented examples. No footage, image, performance or measurement exists. These example choices do not become defaults or user-approved facts in another project.

## Teaching brief

Write a quiet exchange at a repair table. Volunteers can inspect a small object; successful repair is not established. Marta brings an unplugged red desk lamp whose shade sits slightly tilted. Jan receives it. Avoid a sentimental backstory and a light turning on as proof of repair. The two lines below are selected within this hypothetical exercise; no other spoken words are needed.

## Scene, revision A

INT. REPAIR WORKSHOP — DAY

Marta sets the red lamp on the workbench. Its plug lies beside the base. Her fingers stay on the rim of the base.

MARTA
Tylko nie wyrzucaj.

Jan draws a folded grey felt pad toward the lamp. He waits for Marta to lift it just enough for him to slide the pad underneath.

JAN
Najpierw zobaczmy.

Marta lifts her hand away. Jan steadies the tilted shade without switching the lamp on.

## Why the turn can be filmed

The observable change is permission to handle the object: Marta initially keeps a hand on it, then releases it after a careful receiving gesture. We do not assert grief, family history or what either person privately believes. The line asks for preservation; the action supplies a limited response. The ending establishes inspection, not success.

An inferior draft might end with the lamp lighting and “Jak nowa!”. That would add a result the teaching brief did not establish and replace its modest turn. The targeted repair is to remove that outcome, preserve the selected dialogue, and end on careful handling.

## Handoff locks

- Spoken lines: `Marta: Tylko nie wyrzucaj.` then `Jan: Najpierw zobaczmy.` Preserve exact words and order.
- Lamp remains unplugged; plug is visible when that fact is the shot's evidence.
- Felt goes under the same lamp; no extra lamp, new damage or instant restoration.
- Desired camera in the linked exercise: one fixed view. This is an example-specific direction, not a scene-writing rule.
- Timing is not measured. Facial identity carriers are absent; no strict production readiness is claimed.
