# Ad evidence, structure and format

Use for a requested social-ad concept, competitor analysis or campaign family. This optional extension supplies decisions for the existing [strategy pack](../templates/ecommerce-campaign-strategy-pack.md) and [asset card](../templates/campaign-asset-card.md). It does not start another intake. A selected static execution stays with [Static Graphic Design Creator](../../static-graphic-design-creator/SKILL.md); a shared family stays with Commercial Visual Campaign Director.

## Observe an ad before borrowing a mechanism

For a useful small sample, record only ads actually opened or supplied. Use section A of the [creative experiment card](../templates/creative-experiment-card.md); one observation can be enough for a narrow request.

Separate three layers:
- **Observed:** source URL or supplied file, ad identifier when visible, access date, market/filter context, observed active dates, medium/placement, exact claim, hook, dominant element, reading order, visible proof device and CTA/destination.
- **Inferred:** intended audience situation, persuasive mechanism and a possible reason to test it. Label each as a hypothesis.
- **Unknown:** conversions, spend, profitability, targeting or any other field not evidenced in the inspected source. Active status, longevity, repeated use and polished design do not prove a winner.

Text retrieval supports wording, not visual hierarchy. Layout judgments need the actual image; motion judgments need the actual clip. Mark partial access. A supplied performance report can support only the outcome and scope it actually measures. It does not establish which creative feature caused that outcome.

Summarize the recurring convention and a plausible unmet communication need. Turn these into an original hypothesis for this client's product and evidence. Keep competitor copy, distinctive composition, identity, likeness and imagery out of production inputs unless independently authorized for the specific use. A teardown records a research source, not a product reference or a proven baseline. Do not make an absence claim about a whole market from a few ads.

## Choose a communication job, then an eligible format

Name the primary job: explain use, establish relevant proof, announce a real offer, address an objection or create recognition. Choose the structure from available evidence before choosing its visual style. A headline-led concept can be appropriate without a photograph or a button.

| Structure | Evidence required before final copy or production | Useful reading plan |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Product with feature callouts | Applicable SKU and verified features; actual product reference for likeness | Product/action, one decisive feature, supporting detail |
| One number with context | Claim-ledger entry with source, denominator, period, population and applicable conditions | Number and what it measures together; readable qualification |
| Customer quotation | Authentic words, source/context and relevant permission; any objective claim inside the quote still needs substantiation | Quote, attribution, product/context; no invented stars or badges |
| Comparison | Comparable items, verified attributes, same basis/date and required qualifiers | Decision criterion, fair alternatives, supported difference |
| Offer or bundle | Confirmed inclusions, price/currency, eligibility, dates and material conditions | Offer, what is included, conditions, next action |
| Product demonstration or process | Supported operation and source assets; observed or clearly illustrative status | Action and useful consequence; no synthetic outcome presented as real evidence |
| Statement or typographic announcement | Approved wording; factual promises supported; actual event/offer details where relevant | One takeaway, necessary practical information, brand/action |
| Founder or practitioner voice | Real attributed statement, verified role and appropriate permission | Point of view, identity/context, relevant product or process |

This is a selection aid, not a format quota or a conversion ranking. If evidence is missing, choose a supported structure, omit the claim or hold the dependent asset. Do not invent proof or make an unsupported claim acceptable merely by weakening its wording. A quote does not prove efficacy. A synthetic presenter or generated before/after pair does not prove customer experience. Sensitive categories need current applicable policy review before choosing the format.

Carry a compact decision into the existing asset card:
- Primary job, selected structure and why this evidence supports it.
- Claim IDs, source aliases/revisions, qualifiers and copy-approval status.
- First read, image/type relationship and one intended audience action.
- Destination continuity: same offer, factual promise and fulfillment of the CTA; identical wording is not mandatory when meaning and conditions remain intact.
- Placement and actual QA observables.

The static owner chooses original composition, typography and style through its complete existing method. No universal six-word headline, photographic treatment, color ban or default aspect ratio is added. Do not redraw protected identity or treat prompt percentages as verified output geometry. Verify changing placement specifications when needed; this reference certifies no platform dimensions or safe-zone constants.

## From observations to a comparison

For exploration, compare distinct persuasive mechanisms within the requested count; several creative features may differ. For an isolated-variable test, name exactly what changes and what stays fixed, including delivery conditions. Changing static to video, ratio and placement together creates a package comparison, not proof about any one feature. A color change may be a legitimate isolated hypothesis, but is not a new strategic concept.

Use sections B and C of the experiment card only when a planned test or supplied result warrants them. Keep the existing measurement rules and [results feedback method](creative-performance-feedback.md) authoritative. A ready comparison is not permission to generate, publish or spend.
