# Campaign product and human asset production

Apply to the requested campaign asset after its business role, direction and source basis are known. Reuse the campaign strategy and exact locks; do not restart intake or create another production owner. A narrow static request still belongs to Static Graphic Design Creator. The [asset card](../templates/campaign-asset-card.md) is a compact handoff, not a questionnaire.

## Product truth and source roles

Bind the actual SKU/variant and approved product references before claiming faithful depiction. Record source aliases, revisions, what each controls and actual access. Lock geometry, proportions, label spelling and layout, logo, cap/dispenser, material, color, volume/count and included accessories where the real product establishes them. Separate product truth from lighting, set, composition and mood references. Do not invent the unseen rear label, packaging details, ingredients or performance.

Public availability is not a licence or authorization to upload. Record task-relevant rights/permission evidence and verify the chosen operation's actual inputs. If only a text description exists, prepare a concept or conditional prompt; exact product likeness remains unverified and dependent execution waits for a suitable source. Preserve strict requirements rather than silently accepting a lookalike. A generated product render never becomes the factual master for a different SKU.

For perfume, inspect bottle and cap shape, glass thickness/refraction, liquid level/color, label legibility and bottle scale against the approved reference. Choose sensory associations from supported notes or clearly framed metaphor; do not add a visible botanical as though it proves composition. Product beauty lighting must retain readable branding and plausible reflections/shadows. Apply equivalent property-level locks to apparel, cosmetics, home goods and other categories.

## Realistic people and creator status

Resolve the relevant person source as `real_authorized_person | synthetic_presenter | actor_portrayal | Unknown`. Use an adult casting brief when appropriate to the product/audience. Describe observable casting, action, expression, wardrobe, grooming and context without inventing customer demographics or identity rights. For a supplied real person, carry the authorized likeness and source views. For a new synthetic person, describe them as fictional; if recurring identity is requested, obtain an accepted actual reference carrier before downstream independent requests. Repeating a prose description does not prove continuity.

Realism comes from coherent optics, lighting, anatomy, skin texture, expression, fabric, grip and contact. Inspect hands/fingers, body proportions, eyes/teeth, product scale, label geometry, contact shadows and reflections in the actual result. A hand must convincingly hold the bottle; it must not float or merge with fingers. Natural detail is not permission to alter a supplied person's identity or add unrelated defects. Avoid promising flawless photorealism before inspection.

A synthetic presenter is not a real customer. Keep UGC style separate from testimonial truth: a fictional presenter can introduce supported features or perform a demonstrative action but cannot truthfully claim personal purchase, weeks of use, results or endorsement. Do not present generated before/after media as product-effect proof. Real testimonials retain source, exact factual scope and relevant permission. Carry material-connection and synthetic-content disclosure needs for current platform/jurisdiction review; do not certify legal compliance or assume one universal label. Commercial presentation can remain persuasive through a specific use situation, product demonstration and supported offer.

For UGC/video, define the opening situation, credible hook, product action, supported proof, relevant objection and CTA as needed, not a compulsory script template. Carry speaker status, spoken copy, supers, gestures, product contact and shot continuity to the existing UGC, copy, video and audio owners. A proposed voice is not a performed or licensed recording. Review actual temporal behavior for disappearance, changing labels, grip and lip-sync only when the film is accessible.

## Placement and website adaptation

Each asset needs an actual intended placement and role, not just a generic social-media size. Feed portrait, square/carousel and full-height story/reel are composition options; current platform recommendations, technical limits, safe zones and automated crop behavior require the exact placement/surface check. Do not hard-code a remembered size or text limit as current policy. If official guidance is inaccessible, label the export specification provisional and keep the pending verification explicit.

For a website hero, distinguish desktop and mobile crops, focal point, layout slot, image versus live site text, accessibility description and responsive behavior. Verify dimensions and file-weight targets from the real design context or propose them for confirmation. Live HTML text is a site-implementation handoff, not a reason to replace Studio's integrated text-bearing raster policy. If an ad/banner needs text baked into raster output, use the existing native one-pass text-image policy; editable/layered/vector work follows the user's explicit request.

Recompose to retain product, message hierarchy, exact copy/qualifiers and CTA in each variant. Do not reduce a complete campaign to automatic center crops. Distinguish conceptual visual from a deployable export and inspect actual small-size legibility, crop, contrast and visible text. A prompt cannot prove file weight, transparency or device rendering. Hand requested website deployment to an actually available authorized route; producing a banner does not change the website.

## Execution, review and delivery

Planning and prompt writing do not execute tools. An explicit request to generate a concrete selected asset can authorize its available native route; honor known scope without ritual reconfirmation. Before a paid/external operation, ensure provider, input transfer, quantity, cost and retry bounds are covered by explicit authorization. Do not assume account access, billing, attachment transfer or a model from its name. If the requested route is unavailable, provide the feasible brief/prompt and a precise blocker instead of claiming an output or switching providers silently.

For a batch, start with a representative asset when the user has not already accepted the visual system or requested the whole batch. A pilot reduces rework; it is not a new mandatory approval gate for an already specified single asset. Record actual outputs with IDs/revisions and source dependencies through Asset Manifest. Domain QA satisfies the same shared loop: compare actual output against product truth, identity, copy, composition, placement and commercial purpose, repair only diagnosed failures, and stop when sufficient.

Deliver requested available files with their actual status, intended placement, copy/caption where needed, naming and remaining verification. Keep rejected, outdated and uninspected variants out of an accepted final set. A changed product reference, offer, price, claim, identity carrier or landing destination flags dependent cards for reassessment; it does not invalidate unrelated accepted work. Ad upload, campaign activation, budget changes and publishing remain distinct explicitly authorized operations. Production quality and sales effectiveness require different evidence.
