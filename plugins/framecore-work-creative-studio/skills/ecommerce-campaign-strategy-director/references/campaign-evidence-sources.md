# Campaign evidence and refresh points

Reviewed 2026-10-03. These are primary-source starting points and bounded observations, not proof of Studio's sales effectiveness or a permanent platform specification. The website audit, asset cards and staged handoffs are original Studio methods. Reuse current evidence only while its question and applicable conditions remain unchanged.

| Source | Observed support | Use and limitation |
|---|---|---|
| [Meta Blueprint: creative diversification](https://www.facebookblueprint.com/student/path/253130-increase-campaign-performance-with-diversified-creative) | Public course outline distinguishes concepts/motivators and formats | Supports meaningful creative variety. The outline does not prove a campaign's performance or establish an exact budget, sample size or account setup. No course completion or hidden lesson content was observed. |
| [Shopify: marketing reports](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/reports-and-analytics/shopify-reports/report-types/default-reports/marketing-reports) | Reports distinguish referrals and attribution models | Record source and attribution basis when comparing store and campaign reports. This documentation supplies no private store results or permission to access analytics; non-Shopify stores need their own definitions. |
| [Google Analytics: campaign URL parameters](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/10917952?hl=en) | Campaign parameters identify traffic sources and campaigns in reporting | Use consistent campaign/creative IDs. A tagged URL neither installs analytics nor demonstrates that purchase tracking works. Verify the actual implementation separately. |
| [FTC: endorsement questions](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/ftcs-endorsement-guides-what-people-are-asking) | Endorsements must reflect truthful experience and relevant relationships need appropriate disclosure | Supports the boundary against fake customer experience. This is US guidance, not legal clearance for Poland, the EU or another market. Verify applicable local/platform requirements for the actual campaign. |
| [Meta Ads Guide](https://www.facebook.com/business/ads-guide) | Retrieval reached a login/block page during this review | Exact current placement dimensions, text limits, safe zones and account controls remain unverified here. Recheck official guidance or an accessible current account specification at production time; do not treat third-party search snippets as a replacement verification. |

Creative attractiveness, realistic models, source checks and an asset-review pass do not demonstrate conversion lift. Use actual comparable campaign outcomes and state uncertainty. Website claims remain attributed claims until their factual basis is established; a merchant page is useful evidence of its published offer, not independent substantiation of every promise.
