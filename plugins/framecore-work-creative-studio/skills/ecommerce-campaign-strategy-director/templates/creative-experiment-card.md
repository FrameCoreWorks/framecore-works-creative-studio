## Example selection, fictional brief

A client has an approved bottle photograph and a verified refill mechanism, but no customer testimonials or performance study. Choose a product demonstration with one supported feature. Hold review-card and numerical-efficacy concepts until evidence exists. For a hook test, preserve the image, offer, destination and delivery setup; change only the approved opening line. Treat any resulting difference as observational until the actual experiment supports stronger attribution.
