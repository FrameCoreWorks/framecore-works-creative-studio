# Creative production without an Adobe account

Read the relevant section for multi-format assets, batch finishing, local typography, website brand extraction, document review or supplied campaign data. This is applied knowledge adapted from the reviewed [Adobe skill methods](../../../integrations/adobe-skills/README.md). Existing Studio owners and tools perform the work; these instructions do not add an Adobe engine.

## Capability boundary

This route requires no Adobe account, connector, OAuth, asset upload, font activation or subscription. Do not call Adobe initialization or services, offer account linking, or follow an upstream instruction that makes Adobe mandatory, even when such tools are exposed. Do not install the upstream suite to use this knowledge. User constraints take precedence over source routing. Other explicitly selected Studio providers remain available under their own authorization; do not silently use them as a fallback or call paid generation free.

Reuse the current brief, assets, approved decisions and project checkpoint. Inspect accessible source properties before asking questions. Check the actual host for each execution capability. Run only available and authorized operations; otherwise provide the usable crop/layout parameters, edit recipe, review findings or handoff and label missing execution NOT_RUN. A method, tool name or file in the package is not proof of an executable engine. Use host-supported reading/browser tools under their own rules, not upstream browser workarounds.

## Social and campaign variants

The Static Graphic Design owner handles stills; FrameCut owns moving images. Build a compact source-to-output matrix: source ID/revision, requested dimensions and aspect ratio, exact copy, focal subject, protected logo/product regions, intended placement, crop/fit/recomposition choice and status. Read dimensions, orientation, alpha and color profile when accessible. Do not infer current platform specifications; use supplied dimensions or verify the requested placement.

Choose each adaptation deliberately:

- Crop only when the subject, mandatory copy and protected marks survive. Record the crop rectangle or focal position relative to the source.
- Fit the full source when cropping would lose required content. Choose an intentional background and placement from the brief.
- Recompose editable layers when the new format needs different hierarchy. Preserve exact text and logo proportions. A flattened source cannot safely separate its logo just because a layout plan says so.

Show a representative preview of the most different ratios, then apply scoped corrections. Existing approval of the same source, look and layouts can carry forward. Check every final variant at its intended size for clipped text, safe areas, line breaks, subject visibility, distortion and unwanted background. Never substitute a stretch or an unrequested generative expansion for recomposition. Use the existing [exact-copy compositor](../../static-graphic-design-creator/references/exact-copy-compositing.md) and manifest when applicable.

For video this representative-preview rule never replaces the **complete storyboard of the entire film**. Follow FrameCut intake, all-scene/key-state contact sheets, user corrections and corrected-board acceptance before final rendering. Cover per-format framing changes throughout the sequence; a few attractive extracted frames do not establish coverage. A motion sample remains optional on request. Bind approval to current board/timeline revisions and preserve accepted locks.

## Batch photo finishing and portraits

1. Inventory originals and group sources by actual light, exposure, camera/color behavior and intended treatment. Identify outliers, missing files and rights constraints. Do not apply one global correction merely because files share a folder.
2. Select representative samples from each materially different group, including difficult extremes. Propose a compact recipe: white balance/exposure, contrast, color, crop and permitted cleanup, with exceptions. Preserve identity, natural skin texture, intentional features and accurate product colors.
3. Produce previews with an available authorized tool; say which files and operations were actually inspected. Label reduced-resolution previews as previews. Get corrections and look approval before propagating a new treatment, reusing an existing valid scoped approval. A request for a written recipe needs no preview gate.
4. Apply the accepted recipe to original full-resolution sources, never to the reduced preview. Keep originals immutable, use revisioned output names and retain source-to-result mapping, parameters, exceptions and failures. Confirm dimensions/profile/alpha behavior of the actual outputs instead of assuming defaults.
5. Inspect representative final results and every exception; check every file for output presence and declared dimensions. Report visual sampling coverage separately from technical batch coverage. Do not call the whole batch visually approved after inspecting one image.

An unavailable retouching/segmentation engine is a capability gap, not a reason to promise Photoshop-like edits. Detailed local edit instructions are useful but are not executed image edits.

## Typography, templates and mockups

Use supplied licensed fonts or the existing [bundled font kit](../assets/fonts/README.md). Confirm the actual file, permitted use and renderer availability separately. Render a specimen with the user's exact copy, target size and required glyphs, including Polish diacritics when relevant. Inspect shaping, weight, line breaks and readability; do not silently substitute a missing font or normalize away accents. Font catalog resemblance is not license evidence. No Adobe Fonts activation or premium-font dependency belongs to this route.

Use user-owned or appropriately licensed templates and mockup assets. Map each editable slot to actual text, image and style inputs. For a batch, try representative data rows, longest copy and missing values before propagation. Flag missing mappings rather than inventing prices or claims. Keep editable masters and export profiles. Distinguish a 2D composited mockup from a physically accurate material/lighting render. Do not promise PSD/INDD editing, smart-object replacement, background removal or document conversion without a suitable available engine. Asset Manifest owns source and rights records; Delivery Pack owns the handoff.

## Website brand and content evidence

Ecommerce Campaign Strategy owns website-to-campaign work. Use accessible pages, raw DOM/CSS when available, and actual screenshots through the permitted host route. Bound the inspected page set and viewport; do not report an entire-site audit from one page. A screenshot can establish appearance but not hidden metadata, semantic headings, alt attributes, keyboard operation or live performance.

Capture the **current** brand evidence separately from the **target** direction: color roles, actual font evidence, spacing/hierarchy, imagery, recurring motifs, interaction/motion, voice, exact claims and asset origins. Mark unknowns and contradictions. Reuse [DESIGN.md interchange](../assets/design-md/README.md) for a portable brand specification and the existing website-to-campaign reference for production. A donor reference describes a technique; it does not authorize copying its logo, proprietary assets or page content.

For a requested content audit, report evidence, user impact, priority and a concrete fix for each finding. Check heading order, meaningful links, descriptive image text, reading order, contrast, titles/descriptions, canonical and structured data only where the relevant evidence is accessible. Keep decorative images distinct from meaningful images. Preserve product claims and exact approved copy; propose changes explicitly. Never invent schema facts, promise search rankings, declare WCAG compliance from a visual review, or claim measured page speed without a real measurement. Compare before/after at the same viewport and state, with an intentional-change list. Do not import AEM paths, authoring assumptions, deployment or account requirements into a generic website task.

## Documents, campaign data and handoffs

Research reads actual accessible document content and distinguishes extraction from interpretation. For a specified review focus, proceed without a new menu; ask only if an unresolved focus materially changes the work. Bind comments to exact source wording and page/section references. If OCR or page access is incomplete, state the missing coverage. Highlighting is not redaction. Native PDF/DOCX/PPTX/XLSX conversion, annotations and rendering require actual host tools; conversion aims to retain source content/layout, whereas a requested summary or redesign is new authoring. Check extracted text and rendered pages separately where both are available. Deliver a source-anchored review table when annotation is unavailable, without claiming the file was modified.

For supplied campaign tables, Research and Ecommerce Campaign Strategy establish period, timezone, metric definitions, units, grain, segments and comparable denominators before interpretation. Preserve raw data and report missing/zero denominators explicitly. Distinguish counts, rates, percentage-point changes and relative changes. Rank movements by meaningful impact, with small-sample and overlapping-segment limits. A funnel needs compatible cohorts and ordered events; separate step totals alone do not prove sequential conversion or causal drop-off. Connect observations to a bounded creative hypothesis or test, not fabricated performance guarantees. Reuse [data-led motion](data-motion-and-feedback.md) when turning results into a film. No Analytics/CJA account, segment creation or report API is needed for supplied data.

Delivery Pack records scope, sources/revisions, edits, accepted decisions, actual exports, remaining constraints and the next owner's action. Separate author-facing instructions from developer/operations details when recipients differ. Keep credentials out of handoffs. Output Critic separates criteria, observed evidence, unresolved findings and the user's taste decision; self-grading is not independent proof. Avoid a full administrative packet for a small task.

## Editorial selection without Quick Cut

Use FrameCut's existing transcript, source-clock and timeline contracts. Select moments from inspected footage/audio or a source-bound transcript, preserve context and explain cuts. Metadata or filenames alone do not establish speech content. When speech is unavailable, request the relevant material or make a clearly provisional visual plan. Do not imply Adobe Quick Cut semantic trimming is installed. Continue through the complete board/correction/acceptance gate above and existing final media review.

## Worked decisions

- A wide product image has a logo near its right edge. A centered 9:16 crop loses the logo: fit the full source or use separately supplied editable assets for recomposition. Preview 9:16 and the requested wide ratio; never stretch the product.
- A portrait series mixes daylight and tungsten. Preview one representative of each group plus the clipped-highlight outlier, then apply the accepted recipes from originals. Mark the outlier for individual handling and record what was visually checked.
- A requested local font lacks `Ł` in the exact headline. Preserve `Ł`, show the limitation and propose an available licensed alternative for approval. Do not silently replace it with `L` or claim a passed specimen before rendering.
- A text-only host can read a brief but cannot inspect attached media. Return a useful provisional matrix/recipe and the precise missing input; image inspection and rendering remain NOT_RUN. Do not request Adobe sign-in to continue this route.

For a requested reusable identity file, continue to [Draft from a website](brand-kit.md#draft-from-a-website): evidence first, project approval, then the existing kit schema.
