Types:

- **Revision:** a change within the agreed scope and direction. Counts as a round.
- **Our error:** a mistake against the brief, the copy or the specification (a typo, a wrong size, a missing element). Fixed at once; never counts as a round.
- **Change of scope:** a new deliverable, a new direction after approval, new copy after copy approval, or a round beyond those included. Quoted before work starts; started only after the client's written yes.

When feedback arrives from several people or in several messages, consolidate it into one numbered list, mark conflicts ("Anna: bigger logo; Piotr: smaller logo") and ask the decision maker to resolve them before starting the round.
