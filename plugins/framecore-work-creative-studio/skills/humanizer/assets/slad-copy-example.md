# Ślad: copy without a fabricated promise

Original fictional teaching example. The [shared scene](../../screenplay-story-architect/assets/slad-scene-example.md) is invented; no real workshop, customer or measured result is described.

## Copy brief

Within this example only: volunteers inspect one small object brought by a visitor and assess whether repair is possible. No fee, appointment process, date, address or success rate is supplied. Requested voice: calm and direct, with no sentimental personal story. The scene line “Najpierw zobaczmy.” is not permission to alter either character's dialogue.

## Diagnose the weak draft

“Uratujemy każdą pamiątkę. Znamy historię twojego domu.”

Two unsupported implications cause the problem: a universal repair outcome and knowledge of the visitor's private history. Adding “prawdopodobnie” would not make the second claim true. Remove those assertions before polishing cadence.

## Three distinct candidates, not selected artwork copy

| Candidate | Job and trade-off |
|---|---|
| Przynieś przedmiot do sprawdzenia. | Direct invitation. Names the supported action without promising repair. |
| Zobaczmy, czy da się go naprawić. | More conversational. Needs a clear nearby object/service context for “go”. |
| Najpierw sprawdzenie. Potem decyzja. | Makes the process memorable. Less specific without the workshop context. |

These options are original new drafts. Respect a requested count in real use; do not always produce three. A selected line is handed off verbatim. Do not add “bezpłatnie”, a booking link or an event date to complete the design.

## Same information, different use

For a practical explanatory sentence: “Wolontariusze sprawdzą, czy przyniesiony przedmiot można naprawić.” This carries more context than a headline. It is not an accessibility caption, a verified transcript, a claim of actual inspection or a replacement for the scene's exact dialogue.

Acceptance test: a reader understands that inspection is offered and successful repair remains undecided. The surrounding image must not silently restore the unsupported guarantee by showing every broken object becoming new.
