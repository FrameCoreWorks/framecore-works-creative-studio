## Adopted into the motion contract

**Creative DNA:** record product/subject truth, audience need, intended effect, reference signals, the central visible mechanism and clichés to avoid. Trace choices to the brief or a named reference. Use a compact paragraph for simple work; do not create another discovery questionnaire.

**Style Lock:** retain a revision and actual approval for medium, palette logic, line/fill behavior, finish, typography, spatial/camera/light grammar where applicable, realism, dominant motion mechanism and prohibited drift. Include a Style Key only when an actual approved reference, frame, swatch or profile exists. Specify its authority: style, identity, product, composition, first frame or last frame. One image does not silently control every property.

**Motion economy:** make a dominant visual action carry the message and let supporting motion serve it. This is guidance, not a ban on layered movement justified by the brief. Optional expressive/geometric/experimental axes describe direction; their numbers are not quality scores.

**Repository fit and variants:** inspect installed runtimes and reusable brand/UI assets first. Keep motion work separate from unrelated app code. A generated shot, if separately authorized, is an asset on the approved timeline. For each requested format, recompose hierarchy and safe areas, preserve copy and holds, and review that variant. Do not use an unapproved blind crop.

## Deliberate boundaries

Do not import upstream's fixed premium/minimalist preference, compulsory new style exploration, extra `quality/balanced/budget` menu, automatic dependency installation, or additional owner registry. Existing approved direction and Studio's Quick/Deep and learning modes remain authoritative.

Upstream's schema is descriptive and does not enforce all required child fields, approval evidence, integer frame coverage or overlap accounting. Its seconds-based timing and configurable review limits are not Studio's active contract. Use [the frame contract](../templates/motion-storyboard-contract.md) and the existing shared three-pass maximum; unresolved defects do not automatically extend it.

Motion Canvas, Three.js, After Effects and FFmpeg are upstream candidate tools, not newly installed or certified Studio adapters. Research and use them only when the actual requested project and available tools justify it. Referenced engines retain their own terms. This release installs no engine and makes no live-provider claim.

Source instructions were read as input material. The retained license/notices preserve attribution; the active implementation is the scoped Studio method linked from its existing skills.
