# UGC formats, scripts and disclosure

Checked 2026-10-08. Speaker types, formats and script structure are stable craft. Platform specifications and disclosure rules change; each carries its source and check date. A client's, platform's or market's current requirement wins. This reference is a production starting point, not legal advice; send a legal question about a specific campaign to the user's counsel.

Hook mechanisms, retention and capture realism are in [video craft: UGC, short form and original hooks](../../video-prompt-architect/references/video-craft.md#18-ugc-short-form-and-original-hooks); this file does not repeat them.

## 1. Decide who is speaking first

The speaker type decides what the script may say and what must be disclosed.

| Speaker | May say | Must not imply | Disclosure |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Real customer, testimony supplied with consent | Their own words and experience; edits approved by them | Results beyond what they reported; typicality without evidence | The incentive, if any (free product, payment, discount) |
| Paid or gifted creator | Their honest use and opinion | Long-term results from a first use; claims the brand cannot substantiate | The relationship, in the content and with the platform label |
| Employee or founder | The brand's own view | Being an ordinary customer | The connection, in the content |
| Actor | Scripted lines in a dramatisation | Actual use, ownership or results as personal fact | That it is a dramatisation when it could pass for a real customer |
| Synthetic presenter or cloned voice | Scripted, clearly fictional lines | Being a real person, a customer or someone who used the product | That it is AI-generated wherever it could be taken as real (see section 5) |

A synthetic presenter is never presented as a customer, and a creator-style look does not establish purchase, use, results or endorsement. Fabricated reviews and testimonials stay forbidden in every speaker type.

## 2. Formats

| Format | Structure | Proof it needs | Watch for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Talking head | Hook, personal context, product moment, observed result, CTA | The speaker's real experience or a labelled dramatisation | Reading tone; one claim per sentence |
| Demo or how-to | Outcome first, steps in real time, result on screen | The action visibly working in one take or honest cuts | Speed-ups and cuts that hide failure |
| Unboxing or first impression | Package, first touch, first use, honest reaction | Only what a first use can show | Durability or long-term claims |
| Problem and solution | Relatable friction, failed attempt, product as the turn, visible change | The change shown, not narrated | Exaggerated problem acting |
| Comparison | Same task, same conditions, side by side | Fair, repeatable conditions; substantiation for comparative claims | Rigged conditions; named competitors |
| Objection answer | One objection quoted, answered with proof | Evidence for the answer | Several objections in one video |
| Before and after | Same angle, light and time gap stated | Real, unretouched, permitted material | Synthetic or retouched "after"; health and cosmetic claims |
| Routine or day in the life | Product inside a real routine | Plausible, unforced use | Product overload |
| Reaction, stitch or duet | Response to another post | Rights and the platform's rules for the original | Misquoting the original |

## 3. Script skeleton

A planning heuristic for paid short-form UGC, not a platform rule. Adjust to the brief and the speaker's natural pace. Opening targets and spoken-word budgets for Polish and English come from the [short-form timing card](../../pipeline-core/references/short-form-timing.md); check a script with `vo_budget.py`.

| Part | Job | 15 s | 30 s | 60 s |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hook | One lead mechanism, legible without sound | 0 to 2 s | 0 to 3 s | 0 to 3 s |
| Context | Why this person speaks now | 2 to 4 s | 3 to 8 s | 3 to 12 s |
| Proof | Demo, observed result or evidence | 4 to 11 s | 8 to 22 s | 12 to 45 s |
| Objection or detail | One hesitation answered | optional | 22 to 26 s | 45 to 53 s |
| CTA | One clear action, matching the offer | 11 to 15 s | 26 to 30 s | 53 to 60 s |

Write for speech: short sentences, one claim each, numbers only with a source. Sound-off viewing needs the hook and the proof in picture or captions; use [Caption Studio](../../caption-studio/SKILL.md) for caption timing.

## 4. Platform specifications

Read on 2026-10-08 from official help pages. Check the current page or the ad manager before final export.

| Platform | Specification | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| TikTok, auction in-feed ads (non-Spark) | Vertical 9:16 recommended, at least 540 x 960 px; 16:9 at least 960 x 540; 1:1 at least 640 x 640; up to 10 minutes; at most 500 MB; at least 516 kbps; .mp4, .mov, .mpeg, .3gp or .avi | [TikTok Ads help](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/tiktok-auction-in-feed-ads), page dated June 2026 |
| YouTube Shorts | Up to 3 minutes, square or vertical, for videos uploaded after 15 October 2024 (standard channels); a Short over 1 minute with an active Content ID claim is blocked | [YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/15424877?hl=en) |
| Instagram Reels | Unknown: the help pages did not load on 2026-10-08. Search summaries mention recording up to 20 minutes and no recommendation to new audiences above 3 minutes; confirm in the app before relying on either | Not confirmed |

Use 1080 x 1920 for a 9:16 master unless the platform asks otherwise, and keep faces, hands, product and text inside the platform's current safe area ([platform caption specs](../../caption-studio/references/platform-caption-specs.md), checked 2026-10-25).

## 5. Disclosure

Disclose in two places: inside the content itself (upfront, in the market's language, on screen and spoken in a spoken video) and with the platform's own label. Neither replaces the other. Realistic AI-generated or altered people, voices or scenes need their own AI disclosure as well.

The platform labels (Meta, TikTok, YouTube) and the rules for Poland, the European Union, the United States and the United Kingdom, each with its source and check date, are in the dated [disclosure card](disclosure-by-platform-and-market.md) (checked 2026-10-25; it replaces the platform and law notes of 2026-10-08 that stood here). For another market, research its current rules before writing a disclosure line; until then the requirement is Unknown.

## 6. Handoff checklist

- Speaker type and what they may claim.
- Format and script skeleton with timings.
- Claim IDs with their evidence, and the claims withheld.
- Disclosure lines for the content and the platform label, in the market's language.
- AI disclosure when a synthetic presenter, cloned voice or altered footage is used.
- Platform specification and its check date.
