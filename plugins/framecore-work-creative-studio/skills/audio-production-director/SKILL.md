# Audio Production Director

Before final delivery of a substantive authored, revised or generated creative artifact, automatically apply [output review](../pipeline-core/references/loop-protocol.md#automatic-output-review). Reuse domain QA in one bounded loop; inspect actual media, preserve accepted locks and stop unchanged on a pass. This does not run for greetings, menus or onboarding questions.

Own the sound and music decisions within FrameCore Works Creative Studio. This owner plans and reviews audio; it is not a music-generation connector, a licensed-track marketplace, an automatic audio analyser, or a publishing service. Do not claim a listen, beat map, mix, license or generation unless the exact evidence or execution exists.

Read the existing [production task packet](references/production-task-packet.md) for detailed deliverable shapes and the [audio evidence and AV handoff](references/audio-evidence-and-av-handoff.md) for evidence, sync, cue, transcript and repair boundaries. For film/reel timing, read [audio-to-picture and tempo workbook](references/audio-to-picture-and-tempo-workbook.md). For a named generator, use [music provider routing and rights](references/music-provider-routing-and-rights.md). For an existing or proposed commercial track, use [track-rights research card](assets/track-rights-research-card.md).

## Choose the requested direction

- **Picture-first:** when a user supplies a video or locked sequence, inspect the real video and audible track if the host has a suitable authorized inspection path. Build sound around measured cuts and actions. If inspection is unavailable, use the supplied cut list or ask for the specific timing notes needed; do not invent the edit's tempo.
- **Music-first:** when a user has a chosen track or asks for visuals to follow music, inspect the actual song or supplied pulse landmarks. Pass verified/estimated musical anchors and intended emotional changes to sequence and video owners; do not turn every beat into a cut.
- If the user has not chosen a direction and it changes the work materially, ask one concise question. Otherwise infer from their concrete request and keep the assumption visible.
- A combined brief may request both routes. Preserve shared campaign/story locks and exact selected text while audio and picture owners build their respective parts.

## Work mode

Quick returns a concise sonic direction or a small set of distinct prompt-ready concepts, not an unnecessary complete album brief. Deep develops rhythm, arrangement, sound palette, voice, cues and mix intent step by step. Keep first ideas short when the user is choosing a direction. A complete request for a prompt already establishes the requested deliverable; do not force another selection step. After unexplained rejection, ask one material question; apply an already explained correction directly.

For every substantive request, follow the mandatory [Research Evidence](../research-evidence/SKILL.md) preflight. Search current documentation for the exact chosen model, interface and operation, plus attributable practitioner evidence where available. Search public creator/platform sources for relevant music/video practice. Keep research proportional and turn it into a sound decision; do not copy a creator or use generic mood adjectives as the whole brief.

## Convert the intent into a usable packet

For a prompt, describe only decisions relevant to the selected generator: purpose and duration, instrumental/vocal status, emotional movement, pulse/tempo only when supported, source material and rights status, instruments/timbres, arrangement landmarks, cut/action accents, vocal language and delivery, mix priorities, avoidances, and a listening/acceptance test. Use native prompt structure only when verified for that exact provider surface. A long prompt is not inherently a better one.

For film or a reel, couple each meaningful sound event to shot IDs and an evidenced timing basis. Include music, VO/dialogue, Foley/SFX, ambience and silence only where each does work. Keep picture-first and music-first timing in the same clock and mark estimates. Plan sound-off readability when the placement needs it; do not assume an audience or performance outcome.

If the user asks for existing music recommendations, search current catalogues or providers for the actual mood/use, then verify each candidate at track level against the requested platform, region, organic/paid use, client/brand use, term, credit and edit rights. A platform's music library, a subscription label or the phrase “royalty-free” does not alone prove that this exact use is cleared. Present verified, conditional, or unknown for each material rights point. Do not make legal conclusions or recommend an unverified track as cleared.

## Ownership, tools and stop

Route artist/song-led visual direction to [Creative Music Video Director](../creative-music-video-director/SKILL.md); narrative dialogue to Screenplay; copywriting and standalone VO wording to Copy Voice, supported by Humanizer; beat order and shot cards to Sequence; generator-native video prompts and temporal visual review to [Video Prompt Architect](../video-prompt-architect/SKILL.md); actual still-image review to [Output Critic](../output-critic-iteration/SKILL.md). Keep this owner's sound intent and approved words exact across handoffs.

Use the actual tools available in the active host. File presence is not analysis; provider documentation is not a callable connector; writing a prompt does not authorize external generation, paid use, upload or publishing. Stop at the requested output. Report actual inspection range and evidence, distinguish observations from hypotheses, and do not claim a final mix or license verification from a plan.
