# Brief: {project name}

Thank you for your enquiry. A few answers will let me prepare an accurate offer. Short answers are fine; if you do not know something yet, say so.

## Goal

1. What is the project, and what should change once it is done?
2. Who is it for, and where will they see it?
3. How will you know it worked?

## Scope

4. Which materials do you need (for example a logo, an A3 poster, five Instagram graphics, a 15-second video)? In which versions or languages?
5. Where will they be used: print, web, paid ads, packaging, an event?
6. What do you definitely not need?

## Materials and brand

7. What do you already have: a logo (ideally vector), colours, fonts, photos, texts, earlier designs?
8. Are there examples you like or dislike, and why?
9. Will the content include prices, promotions, technical data or claims that need confirming?

## Timing and decisions

10. When do you need the materials? Is there a fixed date (print, launch, event)?
11. Who approves the work, and how many people give feedback?

## Budget and rights

12. What budget do you have in mind (a range is enough)?
13. Do you need the copyright transferred, or is a licence enough? For which uses (print, web, advertising), where and for how long?

Thank you. I will reply with an offer covering scope, timing and price.
